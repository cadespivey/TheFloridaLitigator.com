Contact Our Offices

Florida has a very broad public records law. As a result, any written communication created or received by Walton County Clerk of Courts & Comptroller officials and employees will be made available to the public and media, upon request, unless otherwise exempt. Under Florida law, email addresses are public records. If you do not want your email address released in response to a public records request, do not send electronic mail to this entity. Instead, contact our office by phone or in writing.

Main Line Phone Directory

When calling (850) 892-8115, select from the following options to reach the appropriate department:

Press 1: Court Services (Including Court Records, Traffic, Child Support, Probate, Evictions, Foreclosures, Restraining Orders, Divorce, or Criminal cases)

Press 2: Official Records (Including: Passports, Marriage License, and Tax Deeds)

Press 3: The Finance Department
Press 4: Clerk to the Board of County Commissioners
Press 6: Tourist Development and Tax
Press 7: Jury Duty
Press 0: The Operator
