John Simon, Chief Judge

John Simon, Chief Judge
County: Escambia County
Type: Circuit
Divisions: E (Felony Criminal) & N (Felony Criminal)
Phone: 850-595-4465
Fax: 850-595-4541
Judicial Assistant: Jane Alford, Jane.alford@Flcourts1.Gov
Address: M.C. Blanchard Building,190 Governmental Center, 5th FL, Pensacola, FL 32502

A. Communications with the Judicial Office

Method of Communication: Email is the preferred method of communication for attorneys representing defendants, especially regarding scheduling hearings.

Defendants or anyone sending any communication on behalf of a defendant should file the communication with the Clerk of Court, felony division, who will electronically send the communication to Judge Simon.

Ex parte Communications: Judge Simon will not discuss pending cases with any party outside of a hearing. His Judicial Assistant cannot take a message pertaining to a pending case from a party, family member, or other person to pass to Judge Simon.

Unsolicited Communications: Judge Simon and his Judicial Assistant are not permitted to give legal advice, which includes suggesting things that might be filed, evaluating the sufficiency of information on a form, interpreting any document, evaluating the conduct of an opposing party or lawyer, or giving an opinion about the likelihood of success of an action in litigation. His Judicial Assistant also cannot recommend specific lawyers or excuse a party/attorney from court appearances (only a judge may grant that request).

E-Filing Portal Contact Information: All attorneys and self-represented litigants must provide an e-mail address to receive signed orders electronically, unless excused. Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.516. It is the responsibility of attorneys and self-represented litigants to update their contact information using Form 2.603 any time there is a change in the e-mail account registered for electronic service.

Response to Inquiries: If a motion or letter is filed with the Clerk of Court or mailed to Judge Simon, a response will be given to the inquiry in writing in the form of either a letter or an Order. Please always make sure there is a legible address on any inquiries to the Court. Otherwise, you will not get a response.

[Other Communication Procedures]: N/A

B. Scheduling Procedures

Court Schedule: When a Motion is filed, it is up to the party who filed the motion to email the JA and ask for the Motion to be set for a hearing.

Scheduling Hearings: To schedule a Motion for hearing, the Motion must be filed in the court file. Once filed, email the Motion to Judge Simon’s JA at jane.alford@flcourts1.gov and include:
Copy of the Motion
Case number
Name of defendant
Copy all attorneys involved

Any additional information (e.g., hearing needed before/after a certain date, emergency filing, etc.)

Judge Simon’s JA will provide a date, and parties can respond as to availability. Once everyone has agreed, the JA will send an email confirming the date and time. Do not file a Notice of Hearing until the JA confirms.

Notice of Hearing: Once the hearing time has been confirmed, a Notice of Hearing should be filed with the Clerk of Court to advise all parties of the date, time and the matter to be heard.

Submission Deadlines: All Motions to Continue for Docket Days must be filed by the Thursday of the week prior to Docket Day. All Motions to Continue must include the language “the State or Defense does not object to this Motion to Continue. ”If the State or Defense does object to the continuance, the Motion to Continue will be heard on Docket Day.

Order of Proceedings: On criminal motion hearings dates, Judge Simon does not have a preference on who goes first. If an attorney has an appointment with another judge in our courthouse or in another courthouse, please let Judge Simon’s JA know in advance so that he will be aware that he will need to call this case first.

Continuance Procedure: All Motions to Continue must state: “The State does not object to the continuance.” Otherwise, Judge Simon will not sign, and the Motion will be addressed at Docket Day.

Cancelling Hearings: If a hearing is no longer necessary, notify Judge Simon’s JA as soon as possible via email. The only way Judge Simon will know of a cancellation is through your notification. If the hearing is cancelled, that time can be made available to others.

[Other Scheduling Procedures]: Defendants may file Motions for Early Termination of Probation in motion or letter form. This should be filed with the Clerk of Court, felony division. The early termination request should include defendant’s name, case number, defendant’s address, defendant's Probation Officer’s name and the reason for the request for early termination. Judge Simon will either send the defendant an Order granting or denying the motion or he will set the motion for hearing. Either way, the defendant will receive a response from Judge Simon in writing to their motion.

D. Submission of Orders and Judgments

Format: Microsoft Word

The proposed order should be named as follows:
Division, case number, name, title of proposed order
Example: J 2025 CF 156 Brown Order to Continue

Proposed Orders must be e-filed through the e-portal in Escambia County. All proposed orders must be submitted in Microsoft Word format. Once in the e-portal, choose from the drop-down menu “proposed documents.” E-file the proposed Order in Word format and a cover letter in PDF format. The cover letter may be the Motion itself.

Orders must include “DONE and ORDERED” with sufficient space for the judge’s e-signature. Do not write the date; it is auto generated.

Submission Method: Escambia County e-portal (proposed documents)

Deadline for Submissions: Please see “Submission Deadlines under B. Scheduling Procedures.”

[Other Procedures Relating to Submission of Orders and Judgments]: If a proposed Order applies to multiple case numbers, separate proposed Orders must be e-filed for each case number.

E. Courtesy Copies of Case Law and Other Documents

F. Emergency and Other Urgent Matters

Requirements: Email and E-file the Motion and proposed Order and mark the email as urgent.

Scheduling: Judge Simon’s JA will contact you to set the hearing once Judge Simon has reviewed the Motion.

[Other Procedures Relating to Emergency and Other Urgent Matters]: N/A.

G. Exhibits for Evidentiary Proceedings

Submission Method: Exhibits for criminal evidentiary proceedings must be presented during the hearing. However, if both the State and Defense stipulate to the Court considering certain evidence prior to the hearing (such as a forensic interview for a Child Hearsay hearing), the physical exhibit may be sent to the judicial assistant for the judge to review in chambers, prior to the hearing.

Format: Exhibits must be labeled in the following format: the State shall use numbers, and the Defense shall use letters.

Deadline for Submissions: Five (5) days prior to the hearing.

I. Setting Case for Trial

Procedure: For criminal divisions, cases are announced as ready for trial at Docket Day, and those cases will be scheduled for Final Plea Day. If the case does not resolve at Final Plea Day, it will proceed to jury selection the following Monday.

[Other Procedures for Setting Case for Trial]: If a case is certain to go to trial and the attorneys prefer, the case can skip Final Plea Day and be put on jury selection from Docket Day.

K. Other Division Procedures

ADA Accommodations: Administrative Order 2015-32 requires that all notices of court proceedings include the following ADA notice (in Times New Roman or Courier, 14-point bold font):

If you are a person with a disability who needs any accommodation in order to participate in this proceeding, you are entitled, at no cost to you, to the provision of certain assistance. Please contact: Court Administration, ADA Liaison, Escambia County, 190 Governmental Center, 5th Floor, Pensacola, FL 32502, Phone (850) 595-4400, Fax (850) 595-0360, ADA.Escambia@flcourts1.gov.

Interpreter Requests: If an interpreter is needed for a hearing or trial, please contact Court Administration to request an interpreter at (850) 595-4533 or Trinidad.SerranoBennitt@flcourts1.gov. Information regarding court interpreting services can be found here.

Decorum: All counsel and other persons attending hearings and trials should conduct themselves in the following customary and traditional manner:
Stand as court is opened, recessed, or adjourned for all matters held in the courtroom.
Stand when the jury enters or retires from the courtroom.
Stand when addressing or being addressed by the Court for all matters held in the courtroom.
Address all remarks to the Court, not to opposing counsel.
Avoid disparaging personal remarks or acrimony toward opposing counsel and remain wholly detached from any ill feeling between the litigants or witnesses.
Refer to all persons, including witnesses, other counsel, and the parties, by their surnames and not by their first or given names.
In making objections, counsel should state only the legal grounds for the objection and should withhold all further comment or argument unless elaboration is requested by the Court.
Counsel shall admonish and discourage all persons (parties, witnesses, and observers) from making gestures, facial expressions, audible comments, or the like, as manifestations of approval or disapproval during the testimony of witnesses, or at any other time.

Information for Pro Se Litigants (Parties who are not represented by an attorney):

Judge Simon and his judicial assistant are not permitted to give legal advice. “legal advice” means suggesting things that might be filed, evaluating the sufficiency of information on a form, interpretation of any document, evaluating the conduct of an opposing party or lawyer, and giving an opinion about the likelihood of success of an action in litigation.
Judge Simon will not discuss pending cases with any party outside of a hearing. Judge Simon’s Judicial Assistant cannot take a message pertaining to a pending case from a party, family member, or other person to pass to Judge Simon.
Judge Simon’s Judicial Assistant is not permitted to recommend specific lawyers or advise a party on how to handle the matters pending before the judge.
Judge Simon’s Judicial Assistant does not have the authority to excuse a party or attorney from court appearances. Only a judge can grant that request.
Communication with Judge Simon regarding cases assigned to him can take two forms:
Written documents filed in the court file (with a copy served on all parties); and
Court hearings
