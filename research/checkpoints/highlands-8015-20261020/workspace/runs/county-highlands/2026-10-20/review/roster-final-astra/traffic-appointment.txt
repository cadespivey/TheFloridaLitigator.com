IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR HARDEE, HIGHLANDS AND POLK COUNTIES, FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO.  7-10.13 
IN RE: ESTABLISHMENT OF CIVIL TRAFFIC INFRACTION 
HEARING OFFICER PROGRAM 
WHEREAS, the Florida Legislature has enacted legislation permitting the establishment 
of civil infraction hearing officer programs in §318.30, Fla. Stat.; and 
WHEREAS, the judges of the County Court for Hardee, Highlands and Polk Counties have 
recommended unanimously that a civil traffic infraction hearing officer program (the Program) be 
established to serve the people of Hardee, Highlands and Polk Counties; and 
WHEREAS, the Florida Supreme Court, by letter dated December 15, 1997, has approved 
the establishment of the Program; and 
WHEREAS, the Program is necessary for the administration of justice in this county to 
assist in relieving the excessive caseloads in the county court;  
It is hereby ORDERED that: 
1. 
The Civil Infraction Traffic Hearing Officer Program has been established in 
Hardee, Highlands and Polk Counties. 
2. 
Daveta L. Williams, Esquire, and Kevin James D’Espies, both independent 
contractors, are appointed and designated as contract Civil Traffic Infraction 
Hearing Officers (contractual). 
3. 
This Administrative Order is effective on August 31, 2026, and VACATES and 
SUPERCEDES Administrative Order No. 7-10.12, entered on July 20, 2026. 
DONE AND ORDERED on this 27th day of August, 2026. 
s/JAMES A. YANCEY, Chief Judge
