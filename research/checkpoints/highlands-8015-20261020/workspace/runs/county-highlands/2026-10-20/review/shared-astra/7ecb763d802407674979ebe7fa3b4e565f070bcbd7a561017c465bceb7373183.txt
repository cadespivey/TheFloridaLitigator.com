IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR POLK COUNTY, FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 3-43-0 
IN RE: COORDINATION OF CIRCUIT CIVIL CASES 
WHEREAS, the Court has determined that there are circumstances that make coordination 
of related circuit civil cases more efficient than consolidation of such cases; and 
WHEREAS, the Court finds it necessary to establish a procedure for coordination of such 
cases; and 
WHEREAS, in accordance with the authority vested in the chief judge pursuant to Article 
V, section 2(d) of the Florida Constitution, sections 40.001 and 43.26, Florida Statutes, and rule 
2.215, Florida Rules of General Practice and Judicial Administration, it is hereby 
ORDERED and ADJUDGED that when two or more circuit civil cases are related due to 
the identity of the parties and the case in controversy, any judge to whom one of the newer active 
cases is assigned may, upon motion by a party, enter an order transferring the newer active case to 
the division of the oldest active case so that the cases may be coordinated. 
This Administrative Order is effective upon signing. 
DONE AND ORDERED in Chambers at Bartow, this 18th day of December, 2024. 
s/JAMES A. YANCEY, Chief Judge
