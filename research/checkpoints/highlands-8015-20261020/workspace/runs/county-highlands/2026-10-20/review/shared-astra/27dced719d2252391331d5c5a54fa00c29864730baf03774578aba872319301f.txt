IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR HARDEE, HIGHLANDSAND POLK COUNTIES 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 1-6.2 
ON DRESS AND DECORUM IN COURTROOMS AND CHAMBERS 
In recognition of the respect that is due the people's courts when in session and the duty of 
the judges to require such respect, it is now, by virtue of the authority vested in me as Chief Judge 
of the Tenth Judicial Circuit of the State of Florida under article V, section 2(d), Florida 
Constitution; section 43.26, Florida Statutes; and Florida Rule of Judicial Administration 
2.215(b)(2), 
ORDERED AND DIRECTED that: 
I. Unless otherwise ordered by separate order, the following dress and decorum shall be adhered 
to by all spectators, witnesses and litigants appearing or being in court or chambers or so near 
thereto as to be under the court's authority or jurisdiction: 
A. DRESS IN COURTROOMSAND CHAMBERS 
1. No tank tops. 
2. No bare midriffs or shoulders. 
3. No shorts except for children under 12 years of age and inmates transported for court 
proceedings from a detention facility. 
4. No bare feet except children under 12 years of age. 
5. No unusually rakish or garish dress that would have the tendency to distract from the 
proceedings. 
6. No hats or caps. 
B. DECORUM AND BEHAVIOR IN COURTROOMS AND CHAMBERS 
1. While court is in session, everyone is required to remain silent except those who are 
presently involved in a case before the court. 
2. No eating, smoking, or drinking of beverages. 
3. No gum chewing. 
4. Everyone will rise or remain seated as the bailiff directs. 

5. No cellular telephones or audible beepers are allowed to be used while court is in 
session. 
C. DECORUM AND CONDUCT NEAR THE COURTROOMS OR CHAMBERS 
1. No loud or boisterous talking, laughing or other noise distractions. 
2. No one will be permitted to wear T-shirts or items of clothing having messages printed 
thereon that would have a tendency to distract from the proceedings. 
II. Enforcement and compliance: 
A. All bailiffs of this circuit are required to enforce these rules and regulations unless 
otherwise directed by the presiding judge. 
B. All attorneys as officers of the court are requested to encourage their clients and witnesses 
to comply with the spirit and intent of this Order. 
III. This order is effective immediately and cancels and supersedes Administrative Order No. 1-
6.1, entered on April 27, 1993. 
DONE AND ORDERED on this 20th day of January, 2023. 
s/ELLEN S. MASTERS, Chief Judge
