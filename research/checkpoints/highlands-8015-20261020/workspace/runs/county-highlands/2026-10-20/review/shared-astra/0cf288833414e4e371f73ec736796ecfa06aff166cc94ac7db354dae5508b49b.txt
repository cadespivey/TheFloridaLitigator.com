IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR POLK COUNTY, FLORIDA 
SPECIAL ORDER 
 
VACATING ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 3-25.0 
WHEREAS, on September 24, 1998, the Court entered Administrative Order No. 3-25.0, 
In Re: Consolidation of Circuit Civil Cases; and 
WHEREAS, Administrative Order No. 3-25.0 is not necessary because procedures for 
consolidation of civil cases are provided for in rule 1.270, Florida Rules of Civil Procedure; it is 
hereby 
ORDERED that Administrative Order No. 3-25.0 is VACATED.  This Order is effective 
immediately. 
DONE and ORDERED on this 18th day of December, 2024. 
s/JAMES A. YANCEY, Chief Judge
