Court Services
For security purposes, please do not bring the following items into the courthouse: knives, scissors, handwork needles, metal fingernail files, screwdrivers, tools, and any other sharp objects, or items that may possibly be used as a weapon. All persons entering the courthouse are subject to search. All weapons are prohibited.
* NO BEVERAGES MAY BE BROUGHT INTO THE COURTHOUSE *
The Indian River County Clerk’s Office is responsible for overseeing various departments that deal with the Circuit and County court systems. All departments within the Clerk’s office can be reached at (772) 226-3100. Additional information about the Indian River County Jury process is available by clicking here for the Jury Department.  Here you’ll find information about summons from the Clerk’s Office and performing jury duty.
