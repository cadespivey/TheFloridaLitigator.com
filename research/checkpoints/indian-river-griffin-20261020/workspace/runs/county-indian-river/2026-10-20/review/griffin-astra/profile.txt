PLEASE BE ADVISED that neither the Court nor court staff are permitted to provide any legal advice. All requests must be made by written motion filed with the Clerk of Court, Domestic Relations Division, or by e-mail with all parties and attorneys included. Do not ask the Judicial Assistant to communicate any message to the Judge.

Administrative Order 2025-10: IN RE: Use of Artificial Intelligence in Court Filings

Any filing drafted in whole or in part with the assistance of AI Technology must comply with the AO’s disclosure and certification requirements. Failure to comply with the AO, including failure to disclose or certify, or the submission of AI-generated false or misleading content, may result in sanctions. The determination of appropriate sanctions shall be within the discretion of the presiding judge, based on the circumstances of each case. This AO applies equally to Pro se litigants.
