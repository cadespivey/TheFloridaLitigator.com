Submitting Proposed Orders
Proposed orders are being accepted by e-filing through the
Florida Courts
E-filing Portal
by selecting the category
“Submit Proposed Order(s) to Judiciary (Court)”
.
Orders must be submitted in Word .docx format.
Cover letters are not necessary but may be e-filed in a case.
Sample
PROPOSED ORDER
(instructions and example codes for e-filing)
For convenience, open the file
DONE and ORDERED and CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE
and click the “
download file
” button on the top of the page. The text found here can be copied and pasted (using “keep source formatting”) into the order for e-filing.
Proposed orders with attachments/exhibits can be e-mailed to
msavage@circuit5.org
in Word or .pdf format and must have the judge’s name and signature line typed in full (no codes here) and the applicable parties’ names, addresses and e-mail addresses typed in full (no codes here) in the certificate of service.

*NOTICE TO THE PUBLIC:* The Code of Judicial Conduct governing behavior by judges forbids the Judges of the Fifth Judicial Circuit to discuss pending cases with the public. Please do not call the Court expecting to speak with a Judge about any case. The Court is only allowed to consider arguments made in the courtroom and in documents properly filed by actual parties in the case as authorized by law and the Rules of Court. The Court cannot ethically read or consider any other opinions or arguments about the case. Communications that do not meet these legal requirements cannot be forwarded to the Judges.
