OFFICIAL RECORD FORMS New Witness Address Requirements on January 1, 2024.
RECORDING YOUR DOCUMENT Starting January 1, 2024, certain documents recorded in Gadsden County's Official Records must include the post office address of each witness in addition to the witness's name. Because of changes to section 695.26 of the Florida Statutes, the Clerk's office will not be able to accept documents submitted for recording without the required witness address information.
