G-2024-009-FG
IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF FLORIDA, SEVENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT IN AND FOR FLAGLER COUNTY
Re: Procedure for Selecting Jury Lists and Jury Venires in Flagler County, Florida
Ref: G-2024-009-FG (Rescinds G-2022-009)
WHEREAS,
a majority of the judges conducting jury trials in Flagler County, Florida, have authorized the use of an automated system to select jury lists and jury venires for both grand and petit juries, and
WHEREAS,
said automated system has been approved by the Supreme Court of Florida (see Supreme Court Administrative Order AOSC23-14 dated February 9, 2023);
NOW THEREFORE, I, LEAH R. CASE,
Chief Judge of the Seventh Judicial Circuit of Florida, hereby rescind Administrative Order G-2022-009 and order the adoption of the procedure approved by the Supreme Court of Florida for the selection of persons for grand and petit jury service in Flagler County, Florida.
TO BE RECORDED
in Flagler County, Florida
DONE AND ORDERED
in Daytona Beach, Volusia County, Florida, this
21st
day of February 2024.
/s/ Leah R. Case
LEAH R. CASE
Chief Judge
