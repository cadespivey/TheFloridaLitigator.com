G-2026-028-SC (B)
IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF FLORIDA, SEVENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT IN AND FOR FLAGLER, PUTNAM, ST. JOHNS AND VOLUSIA COUNTIES
Re: COUNTY COURT DIVISION ASSIGNMENTS
Ref: G-2026-028-SC (B)  (Rescinds G-2026-028-SC (A))
WHEREAS
the efficient administration of justice and the assignment of judges in the county court of the Seventh Judicial Circuit necessitate changes in assignments to the current divisions of the court; it is, therefore,
ORDERED AND ADJUDGED
that the following judicial division assignments become effective July 13, 2026, and remain in effect until further order of the Chief Judge:
