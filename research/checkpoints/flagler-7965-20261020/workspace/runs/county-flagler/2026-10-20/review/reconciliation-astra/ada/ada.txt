G-2026-038-SC
IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF FLORIDA, SEVENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT IN AND FOR FLAGLER, PUTNAM, ST. JOHNS AND VOLUSIA COUNTIES
Re: REQUESTS FOR ACCOMMODATIONS BY PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES
Ref: G-2026-038-SC (Rescinds G-2022-038-SC)
WHEREAS,
the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requires that reasonable accommodations be provided to qualified persons with disabilities in order that they might participate fully in court programs, services, activities, and benefits, and
WHEREAS,
the Seventh Judicial Circuit Court facilitates the provision of reasonable accommodations when requested by qualified persons with disabilities;
NOW THEREFORE, I, LEAH R. CASE,
Chief Judge of the Seventh Judicial Circuit of Florida, hereby order that effective July 1, 2026, pursuant to Rule 2.540(c), Florida Rules of General Practice and Judicial Administration, all notices of
court proceedings
in Flagler, Putnam, St. Johns and Volusia counties, and all process compelling attendance at such proceedings, shall contain the statement(s) attached hereto as “
Exhibit A
”, in bold face, 14-point Bookman Old Style or Arial font.
TO BE RECORDED
in Flagler, Putnam, St. Johns and Volusia Counties.
DONE AND ORDERED
in Daytona Beach, Volusia County, Florida this
27th
day of May 2026.
/s/ Leah R. Case
Leah R. Case
Chief Judge
