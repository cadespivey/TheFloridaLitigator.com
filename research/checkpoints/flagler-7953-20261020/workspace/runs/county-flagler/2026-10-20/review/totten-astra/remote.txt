Motioning for Appearance by Zoom 
1. Litigants and attorneys who intend to participate in a hearing (as 
opposed to merely observe), must appear in person unless a
motion requesting appearance by Zoom has been ﬁled and 
permission granted.
2. Motions/proposed orders requesting appearance by Zoom must be 
separately ﬁled by each individual seeking to appear by Zoom.  The 
Court considers motions for Zoom appearance on a case-by-case 
and participant-by-participant basis, and a participant should not 
assume that they are permitted to appear by Zoom merely because 
permission has been granted to a diﬀerent participant.
3. Motions seeking appearance by Zoom should contain an
explanation for why the participant is requesting permission to 
appear via Zoom.  Attached below is a template proposed order on 
motions for Zoom appearance that can be ﬁlled in by the movant 
and ﬁled at the same time as the motion.  Movants are not required 
to utilize the attached template, but any proposed order must 
contain all of the information found in the template below.
4. Motions/proposed orders requesting Zoom appearance should be
e-ﬁled at least two business days prior to the hearing. Do NOT e-file 
zoom motions/orders BEFORE you e-file your notice of hearing. Do 
NOT amend your notice of hearing if your zoom order is granted. 
ZOOM permissions are granted as to individuals NOT for all 
participants AND NOTICES OF HEARING MUST REFLECT IN PERSON 
APPEARANCE regardless of granted zoom orders.
5. Parties seeking to appear by Zoom are expected to familiarize 
themselves and comply with Florida Rule of General Practice and 
Judicial Administration 2.530.

Zoom Hearing Etiquette: 
1. Be On Time 
2. Dress Appropriately 
3. Set The Right Environment & Background(preferably indoors, do not drive) 
4. Stay Muted Until addressed by the Judge 
5. Get A Good Microphone & Webcam 
6. Make Sure Your Internet Connection Is Stable 
7. Don’t Multitask – Stay Focused 
8. Don’t Eat, Smoke, Or Slurp Your Drink(no alcoholic beverages) 
9. Speak Clearly 
10. Listen & Don’t Talk Over Others 
11. Maintain Eye Contact & Position Your Webcam Correctly focused on your face 
12. Your Full Legal Name, First and Last, must appear followed by your 
case number. No Nicknames.
