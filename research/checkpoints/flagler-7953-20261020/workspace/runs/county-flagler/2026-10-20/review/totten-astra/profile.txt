Judge Andrea K. Totten
Divisions 61 – County Civil, Small Claims and Adult Protective Service Act, Baker Act, Marchman Act, Tuberculosis Control Act, Firearm Disability Relief, and Risk Protection Order cases
1769 E. Moody Blvd. Bldg #1
Courtroom 402
Bunnell, FL 32110
(386) 313-4525
None Listed
Judicial Assistant:
Martina Wolfe
mwolfe@circuit7.org
Flagler County Court Judge Andrea K. Totten was appointed to the bench in 2019. She earned her undergraduate degree from Slippery Rock University of Pennsylvania and her Juris Doctor from the University of Toledo. She was admitted to The Florida Bar in 2005 and previously served as an Assistant Attorney General, an Assistant State Attorney, and as a staff attorney in Volusia County. She currently hears civil cases at the Kim C. Hammond Justice Center in Bunnell.
All hearings are conducted in person.
Please submit all legal documents through e-portal
https://www.myflcourtaccess.com/default.aspx
See “important links” below for instructions on motioning for appearance by Zoom.
Please be advised that neither the Judge, the Judicial Assistant, nor the Clerk’s Office can provide legal advice, Community Legal Services of Mid-Florida can be contacted for legal advice at
https://www.clsmf.org
