WHEREAS, in order to expedite the administration of justice in the Twentieth Judicial Circuit, and 
WHEREAS, the public need and demand require that General Magistrates and Child Support 
Enforcement Hearing Officers be appointed by this Court to better serve persons involved in legal 
proceedings; 
NOW, THEREFORE, I, J. FRANK PORTER, pursuant to the authority vested in me as Chief 
Judge of the Twentieth Judicial Circuit of Florida under Rule 2.215, Florida Rules of Judicial 
Administration, do hereby appoint KENNETH KNIGHT THOMPSON to serve as General Magistrate and 
Child Support Enforcement Hearing Officer for the Circuit Court of the Twentieth Judicial Circuit of Florida, 
pursuant to Fla. R. Civil. P. 1.490 (Civil); Fla. R. Juv. P. 8.257 (Dependency); Fla. R. Juv. P. 8.625 
(CINS/FINS); Fla. Fam. L. R. P. 12.490 (Family); Florida Statute §394.467 (Baker Acts); Florida Statute 
§397.681 (Marchman Act); Florida Statute §744.369 (Guardianship Reports); and Fla. Fam. L. R. P. 12.491 
(Child Support Enforcement Hearing Officer) to perform duties in accordance with applicable Florida 
Statutes and federal law, court rules, and the terms and conditions of the Title IV-D Child Support 
Enforcement Hearing Officer Requirements. 
TIDS ORDER shall remain in effect until amended, terminated, or revoked by subsequent Order of 
the Court. 
DONE AND ORDERED in Chambers, Fort Mye
