WI I ERE AS, the Chief Judge is charged by Rule 2.21 S(b)( 4 ), Florida Rules of 
Judicial Administration, with the responsibility of assigning judges to th~ courts and 
di visions in the Circuit; and 
WHEREAS, there is a desire to distribute the workload of the County Court on an 
equitable and geographical basis; and 
WHEREAS, due to the appointment of Judge Derck Lewis to the Monroe County 
bench on September 10, 2025, cases in the Middle Keys Division ofthe County Court 
must be reassigned; 
It is therefore, ORDERED that: 
1. The County Court caseload shall be apportioned on the basis of the geographic 
division in which the case is filed and the date of the filing. Apportionment shall 
be as follows: 
A. 
LOWER KEYS DIVISION 
All county criminal cases filed in the Lower Keys Division shall be divided 
alphabetically by the last name of the defendant. Defendants with the last 
name starting with the letters A through L shall be assigned to Judge Mark 
Wilson. Defendants with the last name starting with the letters M through Z 
shall be assigned to Judge Albert Kelley. In the event that there are co-
defendants in a case, the entire case with all defendants shall be assigned to 
the judge having jurisdiction over the case with the lowest case number. 
Cases that are reopened due to probation violation charges shall be assigned 
according to the alphabetical division described above. 
All new and pending small claims cases shall be assigned to Judge Albert 
Kelley. 
All new and pending county civil cases shall be assigned to Judge Albert 
Kelley, with the exception ofLandlord/Tenant actions. All new 
Landlord/Tenant actions shall be divided equally between Judge Mark Wilson 
and Judge Albert Kelley. 
1 

All new and pending municipal and county ordinance violations requiring a 
hearing, as well as civil traffic cases requiring a hearing in front ofa judge, 
shall be assigned to Judge Albert Kelley. 
B. 
MIDDLE KEYS DIVISION 
All Middle Keys Division cases, including ordinance violations and civil 
traffic cases requiring a hearing, shall be assigned to Judge Derck Lewis. 
All pending criminal cases currently assigned to Judge Mark Wilson shall be 
reassigned to Judge Derek Lewis. Cases specially assigned to Judge Sharon 
Hamilton, attached hereto as Exhibit A, shall be reassigned to Judge Derek 
Lewis. 
All pending county civil, small claims, municipal and county ordinance 
violations and civil traffic infractions requiring a hearing in front of a judge 
currently assigned to Judge Albert Kelley shall be reassigned to Judge Derek 
Lewis. 
C. 
UPPER KEYS DIVISION 
All Upper Keys cases, including ordinance violations and civil traffic cases 
requiring a hearing shall be assigned to Judge Sharon Hamilton. 
2. For purposes of travel reimbursement authorized and allowable under Section 
112.061, Florida Statutes, the headquarters of each judge shall be as follows:

Nothing herein is intended to preclude the assignment of a county judge to a 
secondary office location or division, nor assignment to the circuit court when 
qualified. 
3. All new criminal cases shall be scheduled for arraignment on the basis ofa 
misdemeanor arraignment calendar that will be published by the Court 
Administrator's Office. This calendar shall specify the date, time, location, and 
presiding judge. If the assigned presiding judge is unable to preside over a 
specific arraignment docket, a substitute judge may be assigned to preside over 
that docket by the Chief Judge. Any case continued at arraignment shall be 
continued to the next arraignment docket of the presiding judge. 
4. Nothing contained in this Order shall be deemed to vest venue or jurisdiction 
rights for any litigant to any particular judge or court, but instead is solely for the 
equitable distribution ofcases between judges.

to any incidental reassignment of cases by the Chief Judge.
