WHEREAS, the Chief Judge is charged by Rule 2.215(b)(4), Florida Rules of General 
Practice and Judicial Administration with the responsibility ofassigning judges to courts and 
divisions in the Circuit, and 
WHEREAS, there is a desire to distribute the workload ofthe Circuit Court on an 
equitable and geographical basis; 
IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED THAT: 
A. The Circuit Court caseload shall be apportioned on the basis ofthe courthouse location at 
which the case is filed. 
1.

Predator cases (ICCSVP), formally referred to as cases reported under the 
Jimmy Ryce Act, shall be assigned to Judge Timothy Koenig. 
b. 
All new and pending cases filed in the Middle Keys Division, including 
Involuntary Civil Commitment ofSexually Violent Predator cases, shall be 
assigned to Judge Mark Jones.

Judge James Morgan. 
All new and pending Involuntaiy Civil Commitment ofSexually Violent

Cases in the Lower Keys Division that are reopened due to a violation of 
probation charge shall be assigned to the appropriate judge based upon the

the defendant to probation.
