Procedures 
 
1) All hearings must be set via email, information needed  
a) Case name/number  
b) Estimate of time  
c) Copy of Motion  
d) Copy all attorneys  
2) Once all parties agree on time/date for hearing, email to reserve a slot. Do not 
send out a notice of hearing until you have received confirmation from Judicial 
Assistant.  
3) Be sure to include the location of the hearing on your Notice of Hearing. All 
Trials are held in-person in 1A, other hearings could be held in-person upon 
request of the Court or attorneys, Virtual hearings are held in 1A/3A, links 
below…

4) All documents/packets must be sent to office a week prior of hearing 
 
Cancellation of hearing:  
1. Please call/email as soon as you learn that the hearing is to be cancelled. If 
your case settles, you MUST still cancel any hearings that you have set on 
the Judge’s calendar.  
2. Only the party who set the hearing may cancel it and it is that party’s 
responsibility to notify everyone, including unrepresented parties, of the 
cancellation.
