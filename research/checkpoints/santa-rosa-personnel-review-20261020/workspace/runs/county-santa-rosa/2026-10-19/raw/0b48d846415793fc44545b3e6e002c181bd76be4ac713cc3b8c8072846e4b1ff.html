        Welcome to our new website!

        We are constantly striving to provide the best possible resources for our website visitors. This new site allows you to do a quick search from nearly every page, including the home page. We have added quick links to some of our most common features as well as news, important dates, and even the latest Santa Rosa County property statistics. Please take a look around and let us know what you think!

        Property Owner Protection Alert

        Please read the following notice concerning solicitation letters to our residents offering to provide a copy of your deed for a fee. The businesses mailing these letters have NO official governmental or legal capacity in regard to your recent real estate transaction. The solicitation letters were not approved by and are not associated with the offices of the Santa Rosa County Property Appraiser or Santa Rosa County Clerk of Courts. Property Owner Protection Alert

        File for your exemption NOW!

        You can now file for any exemption straight from our website by simply searching for your parcel. Once you are on your parcel page, click the “Apply for Exemptions” button. Please make sure you have updated your Florida ID and vehicle registration prior to getting started. Start here!

        Online Forms

        For your convenience, our website now provides our most requested forms all in one location. These forms cover exemption applications, combinations and splits, variances, tangible personal property, and more. Proving these forms online allows you to print, complete, and submit certain requests for services without leaving your home, saving you time and money. Click here to check out the online forms page now.
