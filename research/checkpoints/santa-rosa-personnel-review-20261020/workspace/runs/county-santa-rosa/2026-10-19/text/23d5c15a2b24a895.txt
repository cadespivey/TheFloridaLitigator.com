Twenty-six circuit judges and twelve county judges serve within the Circuit and are assisted by general magistrates and hearing officers in hearing and disposing of cases. Judges are elected by the voters of the Circuit in non-partisan elections and serve for six-year terms. Circuit judges handle all circuit court cases, including felony criminal, domestic relations (family), probate, guardianship, juvenile (both dependency and delinquency), and civil suits in which more than $50,000 is at issue. County court judges handle county court cases, including misdemeanor criminal, landlord-tenant, small claims, traffic, and civil suits in which $50,000 or less is at issue. Under the Florida Code of Judicial Conduct, judges cannot speak to parties or members of the public regarding pending cases.
