## Civil Checklist (from Civil PDF, rev 1/29/26)

- **Scheduling**: Email assistant; do NOT call. Up to 3 dates offered. Motion calendar for ≤15 min hearings. Bay=Central, Gulf=Eastern.
- **Conferral**: "Good faith attempt" required before most motions (broader than Rule 1.202). Exceptions: dispositive, emergency, post-trial.
- **Submissions**: 2 business days prior via OneDrive drop box. No hard copies unless directed.
- **Proposed orders**: E-filing portal; Word format; DJCMA; .pdf cover letter. Non-compliant = not entered.
- **Courtesy copies**: Not separately stated; e-portal service. Judge's office will not mail orders.
- **Remote appearance**: Zoom; confirm with assistant; video unless impossible; log in 5 min early.
- **Communications**: Email only. Do NOT include assistant in email chains.

## Family Law Checklist (from Family PDF, rev 1/26/26)
