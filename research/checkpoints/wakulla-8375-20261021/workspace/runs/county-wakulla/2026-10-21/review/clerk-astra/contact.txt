Contact Us
All public records requests must be submitted to the Clerk’s Public Records Officer. Public Records Requests can be emailed to
receptionist@wakullaclerk.com
or you may call 850-926-0300 to make a verbal public records request. Your request can be mailed to:
Greg James, CFCC
Clerk of Circuit Court & Comptroller
Wakulla County, Florida
3056 Crawfordville Hwy.
Crawfordville, FL 32327
Florida has a very broad public records law.  Most written communications to or from governmental officials are public records available to the public and media upon request.  Your e-mail and fax communications may therefore be subject to public disclosure. Under Florida law, e-mail addresses are public records. If you do not want your e-mail address released in response to a public records request, do not send electronic mail to the Clerk’s Office. Instead contact our office by phone or in writing.
We welcome your comments and suggestions regarding the Wakulla County Clerk of Court & Comptroller's Office. Your feedback is very important to us not only in our continued efforts to improve your experience but to provide you with excellent customer service. Please contact us at
receptionist@wakullaclerk.com.

Hours of Operation
The Clerk’s Office is open for business Monday – Friday except for holidays.
Please see the Clerk’s
Holiday Schedule
for the days the Clerk’s Office will be closed.
The Courts Department is open 8 A.M. – 4 P.M. Monday – Friday. Due to a reduction in funding the Courts & Traffic Division closes at 4:00pm daily.  Anyone with a deadline for filing or paying who comes to the Wakulla County Courthouse between 4-5pm will not be considered late if they file/pay the next day before closing (at 4PM).
The Official Records Department is open 8 A.M. to 5 P.M. Monday – Friday. Due to time constraints, passport applications will not be accepted or processed after 4 P.M.
The Finance Department is open 8 A.M. to 5 P.M. Monday – Friday.
