WHEREAS, the Chief Judge is responsible for the administration of the courts within 
the Sixteenth Judicial Circuit, as provided for in Florida Rules of Judicial Administration 2.215; 
and 
WHEREAS, there is a desire on the part of the judiciary of the Sixteenth Judicial Circuit 
to create a uniform policy regarding the use of personal communication devices in 
courthouse facilities throughout the circuit; 
NOW, THEREFORE, it is ORDERED as follows: 
I. No person shall use audible pagers, cellular telephones or any other personal 
communication devices in any courtroom, hearing room or chambers during court 
proceedings in the Sixteenth Judicial Circuit. 
2. All audible pagers, cellular telephones and other personal communication devices 
must be in the off position during all court proceedings in the Sixteenth Judicial 
Circuit. 
3. This order shall not limit the authority of the presiding judge to make such limited 
exceptions to this order as are appropriate and necessary, in his or her discretion, on a 
case by case basis. 

4. Violation of this order may subject the offender to contempt of court and may also 
result in the confiscation of any such devices in order to facilitate the orderly 
administration of justice without undue interruption. 
5. This order shall take effect immediately upon execution.
