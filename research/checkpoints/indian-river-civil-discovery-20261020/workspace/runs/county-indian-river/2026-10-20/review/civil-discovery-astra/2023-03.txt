ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER 2023-03 
 
RE:  CIVIL MATTERS TO BE REFERRED TO GENERAL MAGISTRATE 
 
WHEREAS, Rule 2.215 of the Florida Rules of Judicial Administration requires the 
Chief Judge of the circuit, consistent with branch-wide policies, to direct the formation and 
implementation of policies and priorities for the operation of all courts and officers within 
the circuit; and  
 
WHEREAS, as a result of, inter alia, recent legislative changes1, branch directives2, 
and increased case filings within the civil division of the courts, additional resources are 
required within the division in order to effectively manage the workload of the division to 
promote the prompt and efficient administration of justice; and  
 
WHEREAS, it has been determined that in order to facilitate the marshalling of civil 
cases from filing to disposition, the appointment of General Magistrates is deemed integral 
to the process. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE in accordance with the authority vested in the chief judge by 
Article V, section 2(d) of the Florida Constitution, section 43.26, Florida Statutes, and Florida 
Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.215, it is hereby ORDERED:  
 
1. General Magistrates within the circuit may be utilized for the purpose of hearing 
civil matters as agreed to by the parties or as referred by the assigned judge. 
 
2. As referrals to a General Magistrate require the consent of the parties, should a party not 
consent to the use of a General Magistrate the party must file a written objection to the 
referral with the clerk of court, the presiding judge, and the opposing party(ies) prior to the 
commencement of the hearing on the issue(s) referred.  Failure to file an objection shall 
constitute consent to the referred issue(s) being heard by the General Magistrate.   
 
3. The General Magistrate shall not be referred nor shall the General Magistrate hear: 
a. Jury trials 
b. Bench trials 
c. Final Hearings
