Mug shots and arrest records on websites The Clerk’s Office does not sell nor post arrest records and mug shots to any websites.  Arrest records are public records and arrest record and mug shot websites pull the data from records custodians around the nation to post on their websites, regardless of the arrest’s final status.  The Clerk’s Office has no control over these websites nor what appears on them.  If you see a record on a non-Suwannee County website that you think does not belong there or is inaccurate, you need to contact the website that is displaying it.  You will usually find a “record maintenance” or “contact us” link somewhere on these websites to request correction or removal.  The Clerk’s Office is unable to assist you with this.
