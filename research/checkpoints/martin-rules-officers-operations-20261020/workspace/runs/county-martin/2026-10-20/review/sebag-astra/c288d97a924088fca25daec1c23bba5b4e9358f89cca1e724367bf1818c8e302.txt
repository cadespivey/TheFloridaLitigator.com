AMENDED ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER 2022-04 
 
RE: APPOINTMENT OF JESSICA SEBAG AS CIVIL TRAFFIC INFRACTION AND 
PARKING TICKET VIOLATIONS HEARING OFFICER FOR INDIAN RIVER, MARTIN, 
ST. LUCIE AND OKEECHOBEE COUNTIES  
 
 
 
Pursuant to the authority vested in me as Chief Judge of the Nineteenth Judicial 
Circuit Court of Florida under Florida Rule of Judicial Administration 2.215 and Florida 
Rule of Traffic Court 6.630, Jessica Sebag is hereby appointed as Civil Traffic Infraction 
and Parking Ticket Violations Hearing Officer (CTIHO) for Indian River County, Martin 
County, St. Lucie County and Okeechobee County in the Nineteenth Judicial Circuit of 
Florida to hear any and all matters referred to a CTIHO in accordance the laws of the 
State of Florida.   
Said appointment is effective May 19, 2022. 
 
 
DONE AND ORDERED in Fort Pierce, Saint Lucie County, Florida this 19th of May, 
2022.
