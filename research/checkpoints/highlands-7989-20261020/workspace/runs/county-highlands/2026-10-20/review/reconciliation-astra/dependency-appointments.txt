IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR HARDEE, HIGHLANDS AND POLK COUNTIES, FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 6-10.6 
IN RE:  APPOINTMENT OF MAGISTRATES 
FOR JUVENILE DEPENDENCY PROCEEDINGS 
 
WHEREAS, the Chief Judge wishes to establish magistrate positions in Hardee, 
Highlands and Polk Counties, and 
 
WHEREAS, Rule 2.215, Florida Rules of General Practice and Judicial 
Administration, Rule 12.490, Florida Family Law Rules, and Rule 8.625(h), Florida 
Juvenile Procedure Rules, contemplate such an appointment: 
It is therefore  ORDERED that: 
1. Robin Matis-Jackson, Jennifer Steimle, Philip Vandenbosch, Jeffrey Sirmons 
(effective as of October 1, 2025), and Maria Zucker are hereby appointed to 
serve as magistrates in Hardee, Highlands and Polk Counties for the purpose of 
hearing Juvenile Dependency cases and any other matters related thereto or as 
otherwise referred. 
2. Except as otherwise indicated herein, this Administrative Order is effective 
upon signing and VACATES and SUPERCEDES Administrative Order No. 6-
10.5, entered on August 13, 2024. 
DONE AND ORDERED on this 21st day of November, 2025. 
s/JAMES A. YANCEY, Chief Judge
