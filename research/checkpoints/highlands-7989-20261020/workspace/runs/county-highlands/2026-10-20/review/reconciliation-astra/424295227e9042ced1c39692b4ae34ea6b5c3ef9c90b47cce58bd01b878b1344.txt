IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR POLK COUNTY, FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 3-45.0 
IN RE: CREATION OF POLK COUNTY SMALL CLAIMS AND EVICTIONS DIVISION M1 
WHEREAS, the number of small claims cases filed in Polk County, Florida, has 
increased to the point that a new small claims division is necessary to assist with the 
judicial workload; and 
WHEREAS, in order to promote judicial economy, maximize judicial resources, 
and provide for the prompt and efficient administration of justice in this circuit, it is 
necessary to create Division M1 and reassign cases from the existing small claims 
division (M2) to Division M1; and 
WHEREAS, in accordance with article V, section 2, Florida Constitution; section 
43.26, Florida Statutes; and Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 
2.215; and 
NOW, THEREFORE, it is ORDERED that: 
1. CREATION: Division M1 is created effective October 1, 2026. 
2. REASSIGNMENT OF CASES:  
a. On October 2, 2026, the Polk County Clerk of the Court (hereinafter “Clerk”) 
shall reassign 50 percent of the open, reopened, and closed cases from 
Division M2 to Division M1.   
b. On November 1, 2026, the Clerk shall reassign (as equally as possible) the 
eviction cases from Division M8 to Division M1 and M2. 
3. NEW CASES: 
a. The Clerk will begin assigning new small claims cases to Division M1 on 
October 5, 2026. 
b. The Clerk will begin assigning new eviction cases to Divisions M1 and M2 
on November 1, 2026.

4. HEARINGS AND TRIALS: If a reassigned case has a hearing or trial scheduled 
to take place after entry of this Order, the hearing or trial will be presided over 
by the judge covering the M1 docket.  If there is a conflict, the matter will be 
reset by the judge covering the docket. 
5. EFFECTIVE DATE: This Administrative Order is effective upon signing. 
DONE AND ORDERED on this 1st day of October, 2026. 
s/JAMES A. YANCEY, Chief Judge
