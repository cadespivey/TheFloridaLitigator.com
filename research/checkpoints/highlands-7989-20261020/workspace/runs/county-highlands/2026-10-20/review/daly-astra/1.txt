RULES FOR VIRTUAL APPEARANCES AT DRD-CHILD SUPPORT HEARINGS 
POLK, HIGHLANDS, AND HARDEE COUNTIES 
 
1. All requests for virtual appearance must be made in writing, and must include the following 
information: 
 
a. The case number and identities of the parties at the top of the page. 
b. The name, address, e-mail address, and phone number of the party requesting virtual 
appearance,  
c. The reason why a virtual appearance is requested, and 
d. The requesting party’s signature. 
 
2. All requests must be filed with the Clerk of Court where your case is pending. Please consult your 
Notice of Hearing for the County in which your case is pending. Requests must be filed in person 
at the appropriate courthouse, through the Florida e-filing portal, or sent by U.S. Mail to the 
following addresses well in advance of the hearing:

3. Parties are encouraged to request virtual appearances as early as possible. A failure to request 
virtual appearance at least a week prior to the hearing may result in the Hearing Officer not 
receiving that request. Any request made but not responded to is deemed denied. 
 
4. The Hearing Officers reserve their right to deny any request for a virtual appearance. Attending 
court virtually is a privilege, not a right. 
 
5. No telephonic appearances will be permitted.  
 
6. If a party’s written request for virtual appearance is granted, the party must appear via MS Teams, 
with good quality video and audio, in appropriate attire, in the Child Support Hearing link located 
here:
