IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR HARDEE, HIGHLANDS, AND POLK COUNTIES, FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 5-31.12 
IN RE: ESTABLISHMENT OF CHILD SUPPORT  
ENFORCEMENT HEARING OFFICER 
WHEREAS, the United States Congress enacted P.L. 93 647 in 1975 to establish 
Title IV-D (IV-D) of the Social Security Act, thereby creating the Child Support 
Enforcement Program; and 
WHEREAS, certain laws of Florida establish obligations for timely child support 
enforcement; and 
WHEREAS, Rule 12.491, Florida Family Law Rules of Procedure, provides for 
the appointment of child support enforcement hearing officer(s) (hearing officers); and 
WHEREAS, the Supreme Court of Florida entered an Administrative Order, dated 
February 25, 1998, invoking the provisions of Rule 12.491 for both IV-D and non-IV-D 
proceedings for the establishment, enforcement, or modification of child support in this 
circuit; and 
WHEREAS, the interests of the public, the litigants, and the judiciary require a 
flexible and speedy system for resolution of proceedings involving the establishment, 
modification and enforcement of child support; and 
WHEREAS, a majority of the judges of the circuit court have voted to appoint 
hearing officers for the Family Division as provided for in Rule 12.491, Florida Family 
Law Rules of Procedure, to serve as ordered by the chief judge; 
It is therefore ORDERED that: 
1. 
There is hereby created the position of child support enforcement hearing 
officer.  The chief judge shall appoint one or more hearing officers pursuant 
to the procedures established by Rule 12.491.  The hearing officer shall 
serve under the direction of the chief judge as the court’s representative in 
all proceedings for the establishment, enforcement, or modification of child 
support wherein the party seeking support is receiving services pursuant to 
Title IV-D of the Social Security Act and also to non-Title IV-D 
proceedings as directed by the chief judge.
