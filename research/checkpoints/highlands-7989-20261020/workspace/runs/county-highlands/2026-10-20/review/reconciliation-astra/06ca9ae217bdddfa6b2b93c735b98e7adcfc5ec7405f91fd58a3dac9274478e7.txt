IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR HARDEE, HIGHLANDS, 
AND POLK COUNTIES, FLORIDA 
SPECIAL ORDER 
ADDITIONAL ASSIGNMENT OF CIRCUIT AND COUNTY JUDGES 
TO THE OTHER COURTS OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT FOR THE PERIOD 
BEGINNING JULY 6, 2026 AND ENDING JANUARY 3, 2027 
 
WHEREAS, it is necessary for the dispatch of business in the Circuit and County Courts in the Tenth 
Judicial Circuit for the following assignments to be made; 
NOW, THEREFORE, I, James A. Yancey, under the authority vested in me as Chief Judge of the 
Tenth Judicial Circuit of Florida, in accordance with Rule 2.215(b) (4), Florida Rules of Judicial 
Administration, do hereby assign and designate the following judges as temporary judges of the Courts as 
herein set forth for the period beginning July 6, 2026 and ending January 3, 2027, unless otherwise 
amended. This assignment is for the purpose of hearing, conducting, trying and determining the cause or 
causes which shall be presented to them as temporary and acting judges of the designated courts, and 
thereafter to dispose of all matters considered by them. 
1. Judges Abdoney, Barber, Beato, Bowman, Butz, Carpanini, Combee, Cowden, Denmark, Estrada, 
Franklin, Gravitt, Harb, Gerald Hill, Julie Hill, Kohl, McDaniel, Moore, Ojeda, Pincket, Rafool, 
Ramirez, Sites, Spoto, Swenson, Ward, Waters, Williamson, Winstead and Yancey to the County 
Courts in Hardee, Highlands and Polk Counties. 
2. Judges Bassett (Effective October 1st), Corbett, Flynn, Freeman (Effective October 15th), 
Gibson, Grode, Allison Kaylor, Stacie Kaylor, Pattey, Stamey and Wells to the Circuit Court of 
the Tenth Judicial Circuit and to the County Courts in Hardee and Highlands Counties. 
3. Judge Horton to the Circuit Court of the Tenth Judicial Circuit and to the County Courts in 
Highlands and Polk Counties. 
These judges, under and by virtue of the authority hereof, are hereby vested with all the singular 
powers and prerogatives conferred by the Constitution and Laws of the State of Florida upon a judge of 
the Court to which he or she is hereby assigned. 
DONE and ORDERED in Chambers at Bartow, Polk County, Florida on this 14th day of October 
2026. 
 
 
s/James A. Yancey, Chief Judge
