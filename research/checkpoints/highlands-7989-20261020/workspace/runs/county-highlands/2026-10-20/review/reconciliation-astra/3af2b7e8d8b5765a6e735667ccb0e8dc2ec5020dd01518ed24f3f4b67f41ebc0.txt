IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR HARDEE, HIGHLANDS, AND POLK COUNTIES, FLORIDA 
SPECIAL ORDER 
AMENDING EXHIBITS B, C and F OF ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 1-1.69 
“ASSIGNMENTS OF JUDGES JULY 6, 2026 THROUGH JANUARY 3, 2027” 
WHEREAS, Administrative Order No. 1-1.69 provides for the assignments of judges from 
July 6, 2026, through January 3, 2027; and 
WHEREAS, Brenda Freeman, Esquire, was recently appointed by Governor Ron DeSantis 
to fill the Polk County Court vacancy created by appointment of Judge Kevin M. Kohl to the 
Circuit Court; and  
WHEREAS, Gary Ellis, Esquire, was recently appointed by Governor Ron DeSantis to fill 
the Highlands County Court vacancy created by the retirement of Judge Anthony Ritenour; and  
WHEREAS, for the prompt and efficient administration of justice, it is necessary to amend 
Exhibits B, C and F; it is therefore 
ORDERED and DIRECTED that, effective October 15, 2026, Exhibits B, C, and F to 
Administrative Order No. 1-1.69 are hereby superseded by the attached amended Exhibits B, C 
and F. 
DONE AND ORDERED on this 14th day of October 2026. 
 
 
s/James A. Yancey, Chief Judge
