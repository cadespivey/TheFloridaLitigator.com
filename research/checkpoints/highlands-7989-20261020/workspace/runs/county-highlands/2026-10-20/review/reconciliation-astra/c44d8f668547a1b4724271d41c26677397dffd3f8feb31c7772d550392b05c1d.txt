IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE TENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR POLK COUNTY, FLORIDA 
 
SPECIAL ORDER 
 
IN RE: REASSIGNMENT OF GUARDIANSHIP CASES 
FROM SECTION 17 TO SECTIONS 18 AND 19  
 
 
WHEREAS, to promote judicial economy, maximize judicial resources, and provide for 
the prompt and efficient administration of justice in this Circuit, the Court has determined that it 
is necessary to reassign guardianship cases from Section 17 to Sections 18 and 19; and 
 
 
It is, therefore, ORDERED that: 
1. The Polk County Clerk of the Circuit Court (“Clerk”) shall randomly reassign 50 
percent of the open and reopened guardianship cases from Section 17 to Sections 18 
and 19, with 25 percent of the cases being reassigned to each section. 
2. The Clerk shall reassign the guardianship cases on or before October 5, 2026. 
3. If a transferred case has a hearing scheduled to occur after the date of transfer, the 
hearing shall take place as scheduled before the judge covering Section 17. 
4. This Order shall be effective upon signing. 
 
DONE and ORDERED on this 2nd day of October 2026. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
s/JAMES A. YANCEY, Chief Judge
