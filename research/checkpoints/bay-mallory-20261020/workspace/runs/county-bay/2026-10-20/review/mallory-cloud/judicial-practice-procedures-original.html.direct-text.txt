Our Vision
\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t
Justice in Florida will be Accessible, Fair, Effective, Responsive, and Accountable.
\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t
Our Mission
\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t
Protect rights and liberties, uphold and interpret the law, and provide for peaceful resolution of disputes
