Circuit Civil Division
Circuit Civil Division handles the following:
Cases Which Involve Monetary Judgment in excess of $50,000
Declaratory Judgments
Foreclosure of Mortgages
Negligence
Professional Malpractice and other matters where a claimant may be made whole by receiving a money judgment.
Contracts and Indebtedness
Eminent Domain
County Civil Division
County Civil Division handles the following:
Residential and Non-Residential Evictions
Breach of Contract
Cases which involve monetary judgment from $8,001 - $50,000
Damages
Declaratory Judgments
Foreclosure of Mortgages where value of property is less than $50,000
Negligence
Other matters where a claimant may be made whole by receiving a money judgment
Unlawful Detainer
Small Claims Division
Small Claims Division handles the following:
Cases which involve monetary judgment does not exceed of $8,000
Replevin cases in which property does not exceed $8,000
