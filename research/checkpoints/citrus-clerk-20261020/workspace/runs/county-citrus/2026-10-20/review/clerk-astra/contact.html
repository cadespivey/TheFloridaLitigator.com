  1. By Phone

To reach all departments of the Clerk’s Office, please call: 352-341-6424

When calling, please follow the below prompts: 

  1. for Payments
  2. for Finance, Internal Audit, Official Records, Passports, and Marriage Licenses
  3. for Traffic and Other Citations
  4. for Criminal Matters
  5. for Civil Matters
  6. for Family Related Matters
  7. for Jury and Evidence
  8. for Payment Plan Information

For Injunction questions, please call directly: 352-341-6417 

**If an injunction is needed after business hours, weekends or holidays, call 911.

For Jury Duty questions, you may also directly call: 352-341-6405. 

If you are hearing impaired and need to use the TDD phone line, please call: 800-955-8771
