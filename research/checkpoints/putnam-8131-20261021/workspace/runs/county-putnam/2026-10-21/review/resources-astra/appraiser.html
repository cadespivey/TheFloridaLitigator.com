Late Filing for the 2026 Homestead Exemption

If you missed the March 2, 2026, deadline to file for your Homestead Exemption, you may still have the opportunity to apply.
While our online system is now closed for the 2026 tax year, you can visit our Palatka office to complete a “Late File”
application in person. Office Location: 312 Oak St Palatka, FL 32177

To confirm your eligibility, please bring documents that verify the following:

Eligibility Guidelines

  • You owned your home on or before January 1, 2026.
  • You lived in and established the home as your primary residence on or before January 1, 2026.
  • You do not currently receive residency-based tax benefits (such as STAR, Enhanced STAR, Owner Credit, etc.) on any other property.

Verification Documents

To assist with your application, please provide the following items showing your current home address:

  • Florida Driver’s License or Florida ID.
  • Vehicle Registration.
  • Voter Registration Card.
  • Permanent Resident Card (for non-U.S. Citizens).

Should you have any questions about these items or your specific situation, please contact our office at
386-329-0286.

E-File Notice

If you missed the March 3, 2025 deadline to file for Homestead Exemption for 2025, there still may be time.
If you meet the qualifications listed below and have all the required documentation, you may still complete a
“Late File” application by coming into one of our offices.

  • E-Files and/or manual applications are not accepted for late filing

    • Qualifications:

    1. Own your home prior to January 1, 2025
    2. Occupied and claiming the home as your primary residence on or before January 1, 2025
    3. Have no other residency-based benefits elsewhere (Rollback, STAR, Enhanced STAR, Abatement, etc.)

    Documentation:

    • Valid Florida Driver License or FL ID for non-drivers (residence address must match home address)
    • Valid Vehicle Registration (residence address must match home address)
    • Valid Voters Card (residence address on voters card must match home address)
    • Permanent Resident Card (for non US Citizens)
