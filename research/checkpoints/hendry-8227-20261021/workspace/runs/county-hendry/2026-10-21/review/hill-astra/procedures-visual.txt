ONLINE PROCEDURES
To obtain hearing availability, please send an email to LSavinsky@ca.cjis20.org:
Provide the case style and case number in the subject line
Attach the filed version of the motion or petition, that is to be heard (all motions must be filed prior to scheduling a hearing)
Specify the amount of time needed
Please send all proposed orders in word form. Please copy opposing counsel in all emails.
All hearings are special set.
Please do not file a Notice of Hearing until you have received a confirmation email from my Judicial Assistant.
