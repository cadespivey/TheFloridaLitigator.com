Circuit Civil Division
The Circuit Civil Division handles civil actions in which the damages being sought or the matter in controversy is $50,000 and above.
Common Case Types
The majority of the cases filed in Circuit Civil involve disputes over the title and boundaries of real property, including:
Foreclosure Actions
Quiet Title Suits
Partition Complaints
Condemnation/Eminent Domain Suits
Some of the other types of cases filed in the Circuit Civil Division are:
Medical Malpractice
Negligence
Declaratory Complaints
Contract Disputes
Writs of Certiorari
Writs of Habeas Corpus
Writs of Prohibition
All other civil actions that do not fall within the jurisdiction of the County Court
Due to the complex legal nature of these cases, the Circuit Civil Division does not offer any forms or advice on how to proceed with these actions.
