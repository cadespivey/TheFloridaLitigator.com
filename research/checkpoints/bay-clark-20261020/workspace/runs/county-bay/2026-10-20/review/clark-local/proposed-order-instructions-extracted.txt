Attorney Instructions for Successful Submissions 
of Proposed Orders to Judiciary 
 
The following information is taken from page 2 of Judge Patterson’s memo, dated for  
December 15, 2020, RE: Submission of Proposed Order to Judiciary.  
 
This memo and a training video on DJMCA formatting, video by Judge Garcia, can also be 
found on our Circuit website. 
 
➢ You must use the codes as pairs (e.g., JJJJ & DDDD for Judge’s signature). Below 
are the required pairs. 
a. DDDD = Judge Signature Date/  JJJJ = Judge Signature 
b. MMMM = Mailing Date/  AAAA = Judicial Assistant Signature 
c. RRRR = Reported and Recommended Date/  GGGG = GM or JHO 
Signature 
d. CCCC = Service List 
➢ The codes must be all capital letters. 
➢ You must add all 4 letters for each field. 
➢ These codes should only be used once per line.  
 
Since becoming more familiar with ICMS, we have come to find that there are additional 
nuances that should also be mentioned in conjunction with the DJMCA formatting listed above. 
For example: 
• Do not put spaces in front of the JJJJ, GGGG or AAAA. To move the signature fields to 
the preferred location you should only use the alignment buttons (Align Left, Center, 
Align Right)  OR  use the tab key.  Failure to do this will result in the signature(s) not 
adhering and possible rejection of your document. 
 
• Do not use page breaks or section breaks in your document. This causes blank pages to 
occur in the system. This will also result in the rejection of your document because the 
clerk’s office cannot delete pages once filed. It will look as though a page of the filing is 
missing, even though it was only a blank page. 
 
• Do not insert memos, notes, or comments into your documents. Hidden formatting 
like this will show up in the system and is difficult to remove for the recipient. This can 
also result in the rejection of your document.
