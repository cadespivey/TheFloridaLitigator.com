Proposed Orders eFiling
Pursuant to
AOSC19-74
Judicial Electronic Filing, please read the instructions below. Failure to follow these
procedures
will result in your submission being returned without docketing.
Reference Materials:
Procedure for submitting proposed orders in the Fourth Circuit
How to eFile a proposed order
see Page 141 on Portal eFiler User Manual (MyFLCourtAccess.com website)
Counsel shall ensure the Service List is complete when filing the Proposed Orders. The portal will also require a cover letter/copy of motion in PDF format that should be combined as a single PDF document.
IMPORTANT NOTICE REGARDING SERVICE OF ORDERS (attorney of record)
:  Counsel shall ensure the Clerk’s docket reflects the current attorney of record or change in appearance of attorneys.  The Court’s service of orders is auto-generated based upon the appearance filed by an attorney in each case.
