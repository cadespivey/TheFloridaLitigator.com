Issued
2019-09-26
Key requirements
  • Attorneys use the Portal Proposed Order function except as otherwise directed by the presiding judge case by case or specified by statute, rule or Supreme Court administrative order.
  • Attorneys excused under Rule 2.516 may use paper with a certificate of service, conforming copies and addressed stamped envelopes. Unregistered pro se litigants may submit Word by email or paper with copies/envelopes; only paper submissions include a certificate of service.
  • The directive states specific attorney-service and certificate-of-compliance duties in cases with one pro se party; its required notice qualifies the five-business-day service duty by the pro se party lacking Portal access/registration. See paragraphs 2–3 for wording; do not generalize to every pro se party.
  • Paragraph 4 states that electronic proposed orders no longer include a certificate of service effective October 1, 2019.
Review scope
Pages 1–2 general submission rules and qualifications; historical distribution list reviewed solely as a lead, not current appointment evidence.
Scope
Directive states applicability to parties in civil, probate and family proceedings in Santa Rosa; practical order-submission rules only.
