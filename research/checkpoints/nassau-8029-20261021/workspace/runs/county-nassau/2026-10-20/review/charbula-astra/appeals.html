Fourth Circuit Appeals

Appeals

The Fourth Judicial Circuit has established an Appellate Division to resolve certain administrative rulings and municipal ordinance violations. The Honorable Meredith Charbula is the Administrative Judge for the Appellate Division. The cases are randomly assigned to a three-judge panel of judges from the Civil, Family, Juvenile, and Probate Divisions. Please contact SMeeks@coj.net with any questions regarding the Appellate Division.

Instructions to File an Appeal
