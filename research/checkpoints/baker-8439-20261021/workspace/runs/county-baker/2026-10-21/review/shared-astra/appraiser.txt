Welcome to our Website
This website is meant to serve as an extension of our office and to provide you vital property assessment information for Baker County in an enjoyable and user friendly format that will display properly regardless of the device used.  Our office is charged with appraising all property in Baker County, Real and Personal Property, for Ad Valorem tax purposes on an annual basis.  In addition, we maintain accurate ownership records and administer exemptions and classifications in accordance with Florida Statutes.  If you cannot find the information you need on this website, please do not hesitate to contact our office.
