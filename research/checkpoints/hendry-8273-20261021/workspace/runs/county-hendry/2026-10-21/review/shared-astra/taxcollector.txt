Most Frequently Asked Questions
When do my taxes become delinquent?
Taxes become delinquent on April 1st.
I do not agree with the assessed value or taxable value. Who do I talk to?
The Property Appraiser's office is responsible for the assessed value. Please contact their office at 863-675-5270 or visit their website at
www.hendryprop.com
Do you honor the postmark for property tax payments?
Yes, for the period of November 1 through March 31. Even if we do not receive your payment until the next month, we will honor the postmark of the United States Postal Service.
If I am not the property owner and I pay the taxes do I get ownership of the property?
No, anyone may pay the taxes, but this does not give you ownership rights of the property.
