SECOND AMENDED ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER 2017-13 
 
RE: APPOINTMENT OF JEFFREY P. BATTISTA AS CIVIL TRAFFIC INFRACTION 
AND PARKING TICKET VIOLATIONS HEARING OFFICER FOR INDIAN RIVER, 
MARTIN, ST. LUCIE AND OKEECHOBEE COUNTIES 
 
 
 
Pursuant to the authority vested in me as Chief Judge of the Nineteenth Judicial 
Circuit Court of Florida under Florida Rule of Judicial Administration 2.215 and Florida 
Rule of Traffic Court 6.630, JEFFREY P. BATTISTA is hereby appointed as Civil Traffic 
Infraction and Parking Ticket Violations Hearing Officer (CTIHO) for Indian River County, 
Martin County, St. Lucie County and Okeechobee County in the Nineteenth Judicial 
Circuit of Florida to hear any and all matters referred to a CTIHO in accordance the laws 
of the State of Florida.   
 
 
DONE AND ORDERED in Fort Pierce, Saint Lucie County, Florida this 19th of May, 
2022, nunc pro tunc to May 1, 2021.
