Civil Case Management
The Florida Supreme Court Issued Administrative Order
AOSC20-23 amendment 10
on March 9, 2021 requiring the Chief Judge to implement a differentiated active case management system (DACMS) for most civil cases. The most recent Florida Supreme Court Administrative Order regarding DACMS is
AOSC2023-0962
.
In compliance with the Supreme Court Order, Chief Judge Daniel B. Merritt, Jr. issued administrative order
A-2024-59
. This order creates a differentiated case management system DACMS for the Fifth Judicial Circuit. You can find the order and forms associated with the order by following the links on this page.
For questions and concerns, contact our Program Coordinator, Tracie Boyette at
352-253-1624
