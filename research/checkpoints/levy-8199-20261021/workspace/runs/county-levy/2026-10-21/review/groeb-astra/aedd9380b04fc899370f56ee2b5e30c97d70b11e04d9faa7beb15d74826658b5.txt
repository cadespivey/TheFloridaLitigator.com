Doe – 2-Hour Hearing Requested.). Ex parte Communications: When you are represented by Counsel, contact your attorney.  All communications with the judicial office must comply with Canon 3 of the Code of Judicial Conduct, which prohibits a judge from initiating, permitting, or considering ex parte communications and from considering other communications outside the presence of the parties concerning a pending or impending proceeding, unless authorized by law. All parties must be copied on any e-mail directed to the judicial office, unless an ex parte communication is authorized by law. Unsolicited Communications: Unsolicited communications from non-parties will not be considered by the court. Parties may only contact the judicial office in accordance with these practices and procedures. E-Filing Portal Contact Information: All attorneys and self-represented litigants must provide an e-mail address to receive signed orders electronically, unless excused. Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.516. It is the responsibility of attorneys and self-represented litigants to update their contact information using Form 2.603 any time there is a change in the e-mail account registered for electronic service. Response to Inquiries: Judicial Assistants are not authorized to provide legal advice. When you contact the Judge’s office please know letters, e-mails and other correspondence are all public record and will be recorded. Remote Appearance Remote Appearance Platform Used & Procedure: The Eighth Judicial Circuit has adopted the Zoom platform to conduct remote hearings. Judge Groeb’s office is currently using this platform for remote status conferences, pre-trial conferences and other non-evidentiary hearings only. When these proceedings are conducted via Zoom, the Court will be hosting the hearings and there is no cost to any participant. All participants will need a computer with a web cam and microphone (preferred) or a tablet with Internet to access the Zoom application. Please make arrangements well in advance of your hearing to secure a location with adequate Internet/Wi-Fi connectivity. You can download the application and register for a free account at www.zoom.com . PLEASE NOTE: THE GUIDELINES BELOW ARE GENERAL GUIDELINES OFFERED TO PROVIDE THE PARTIES WITH AN OVERVIEW OF HOW A ZOOM HEARING MIGHT WORK. IN EACH INDIVIDUAL CASE, AN ORDER SETTING HEARING, WITH SPECIFIC ZOOM INSTRUCTIONS TO YOUR PARTICULAR CASE WILL BE ENTERED. IF THERE IS A CONFLICT BETWEEN THESE GUIDELINES AND THE ORDER ENTERED IN YOUR CASE, YOU SHOULD FOLLOW THE INSTRUCTIONS CONTAINED IN THE SPECIFIC ORDER ENTERED IN YOUR CASE. Platform Meeting ID#: Once a hearing time is scheduled, you will receive a link to a Zoom hearing (in the Order Setting Hearing via Zoom). The Zoom hearing information shall be provided only to third parties who are anticipated to participate in the hearing and shall NOT be publicly published . Scheduling a Status Conference/Non-Evidentiary Hearing by Zoom: Please email the Judicial Assistant, Alexandra Courtney, at courtneya@circuit8.org to to schedule your hearing. You must inform her that you are requesting a zoom hearing in order for a Zoom hearing to be set up . You will need to provide the case number, subject of the hearing, amount of time requested, and the names and email addresses of all attorneys and/or parties who will be participating. Once a hearing time is scheduled, you will receive a link to a Zoom hearing (in the Order Setting Hearing via Zoom). All parties should use the link to join the hearing prior to the start time. All participants shall enter their full name, when prompted. Participants may be entered into a virtual waiting room, and they will be brought into the hearing when their case is called. Due to high call volume, please expect delays and remain on the line until your case is called. Appearance of Parties: The attorney or party requesting the Zoom hearing will be responsible for sharing the Zoom hearing information with any third party and for making arrangements for their appearance, including, but not limited to, court reporters, witnesses, etc. The court will not be able to call parties to include them in the hearing. The Zoom hearing information shall be provided only to third parties who are anticipated to participate in the hearing and shall NOT be publicly published . Each party will need to provide the court with a list of all parties, witnesses, etc. who the party expects to participate in the Zoom hearing, no less than three (3) business days prior to the hearing. This information shall be provided via email to courtneya@circuit8.org . Decorum and Attire: Although the hearing is being conducted virtually, proper courtroom decorum should be maintained. Participants should be in a quiet setting and minimize any external distractions. Be aware of what is behind you and choose a solid neutral wall, if possible. Please dress like you are coming to the courthouse. Preparation: Preparation and communication are the keys to a successful remote hearing. Counsel, and or the parties, should communicate with each other prior to the hearing and attempt to resolve as many issues as possible outside of court. Submission of Orders and Judgments Format: All proposed orders must be submitted through the Florida Courts E-Filing portal in Microsoft Word format. A courtesy copy of the motion, and a cover letter (if any), should accompany the proposed order. All orders shall have a 2″ x 3.5″ blank space on the first page of the order in the upper right-hand corner. An example of an order with this space requirement can be found here . All orders shall include a certificate of service at the bottom of the order.  Please indicate the name of the party who is to receive the order, as well as that party’s e-mail/e-service address OR mailing address, if a hard copy is required. If there are any parties to the case requiring service by US mail, attorneys shall submit pre-addressed, postage paid envelopes to Judge Groeb’s office along with copies of the order for conforming. Please make a note of the case number on the envelope . Submission Method: ALL Proposed Orders and Motions should be submitted via e-filing by the Alachua Clerk of Court . This excludes warrants/arrest/capias, which should be emailed to the judicial assistant at courtneya@circuit8.org . Emergency and Other Urgent Matters Scheduling: Please note that emergency hearings will be set by the Court without being coordinated with counsel and/or parties. Pretrial Procedures and Conferences Status Conference: Please note that status conferences will be set by the Court without being coordinated with counsel and/or parties.
