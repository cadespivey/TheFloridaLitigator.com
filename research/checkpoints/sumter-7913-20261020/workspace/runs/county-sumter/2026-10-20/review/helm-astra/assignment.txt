IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE FIFTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
IN AND FOR SUMTER COUNTY, FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO.: S-2025-58-A 
AMENDED ORDER OF JUDICIAL ASSIGNMENTS FOR SUMTER COUNTY 
CIRCUIT JUDGES, COUNTY JUDGES, GENERAL MAGISTRATE AND 
HEARING OFFICER FOR THE YEAR 2026 
THIS CAUSE having come to the attention of the Court as one which 
affects the efficient administration of justice within Sumter County, and the 
Court having the authority to enter administrative orders pertaining to the 
efficient administration of justice, and the Court being advised, it is therefore 
ORDERED as follows: 
1.
This Order shall be effective as follows:
a. As to the County Court, General Magistrate, and Hearing officer
assignments (paragraphs IV, V, VI, and VII), Monday, June 1st,
2026;
b. As to the Circuit Court assignments (paragraphs I, II, and III),
Monday, June 29, 2026.
2.
Assignment of Judicial duties among the Judges and Judicial
Officers of Sumter County shall be as follows: 
I.
The Honorable Charles L. W. Helm, Administrative Judge and
Circuit Judge of Sumter County, is hereby assigned the following cases: 
•
All Felony cases;
•
Juvenile Delinquency;
•
Jimmy Ryce Cases.
