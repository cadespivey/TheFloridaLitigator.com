IN THE COURTS OF THE FIRST JUDICIAL CIRCUIT
IN AND FOR THE STATE OF FLORIDA
Administrative Order No. 2014-41
IN RE:
MAGISTRATES
ORDER APPOINTING MAGISTRATE MICHELLE A. INERE
WHEREAS, the Legislature has created magistrate positions for the First Judicial
Circuit;
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that I, Terry D. Terrell, pursuant to
authority vested in me as Chief Judge of the First Judicial Circuit do hereby appoint Michelle A. 
Inere as Magistrate for the First Judicial Circuit effective September 2, 2014 to serve in all areas
of responsibility authorized pursuant to Florida court rules and statutes.
The Magistrate is required to take the appropriate oath and said instrument shall be duly
recorded.
DONE AND ORDERED at Pensacola, Escambia County, Florida this 29th day of
August, 2014.
/S/ TERRY D. TERRELL
