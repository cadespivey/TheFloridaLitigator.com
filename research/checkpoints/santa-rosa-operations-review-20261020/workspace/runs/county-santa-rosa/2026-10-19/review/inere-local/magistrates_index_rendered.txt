Magistrates/Hearing Officers

In Florida's trial court system, magistrates assist judges by handling specific matters to manage the court's workload. They conduct hearings, make findings of fact, and provide recommendations on issues such as family law, child support, and custody disputes. Magistrates also draft proposed orders for judges to review. Their recommendations are subject to a judge's approval, ensuring that final judicial authority remains with the judges.

Child support hearing officers specialize in child support matters, conducting hearings and making recommendations on child support obligations to expedite resolution within the judicial system. All hearing officers' decisions are subject to judicial review to uphold the integrity of the court's oversight and legal standards.
