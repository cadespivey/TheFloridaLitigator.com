Court Security/Emergency Procedures

The safety and security of our employees, litigants, and the public are of utmost importance. Everyone entering a court facility in the First Judicial Circuit will pass through a security screening checkpoint. When planning your visit to a court facility, please allow ample time to pass through the checkpoint.

To expedite the security screening process, please do NOT attempt to enter any court facility with any of the below items:

Weapons or firearms. This includes sharp items that could be used as weapons, including knives, scissors, needles, metal fingernail files, screwdrivers, and similar tools.

Destructive or explosive devices.

Hazardous materials, including any chemical devices.
