Santa Rosa County Courthouse

Milton, FL 32583

Hours of Operation
Monday through Friday: 8:00 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.
Closed on weekends and court-observed holidays.


Parking: Free public parking is available along the frontage of the Santa Rosa County Courthouse, unless otherwise noted with employee parking signs. Accessible parking for persons with disabilities is available in front of the courthouse, as well as in the employee parking lot along the side of the courthouse.
