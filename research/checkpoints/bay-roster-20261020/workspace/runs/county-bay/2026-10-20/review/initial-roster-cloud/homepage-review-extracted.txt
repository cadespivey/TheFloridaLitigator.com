Welcome from the Chief Judge
Christopher N. Patterson
Welcome to Florida's Fourteenth Judicial Circuit, which includes
the six counties of Bay, Calhoun, Gulf, Holmes, Jackson, and
Washington. The Fourteenth Judicial Circuit was
created in 1915. It is the mission of Florida's independent
Judiciary to protect rights and liberties, uphold
and interpret the law, and provide for peaceful
resolution of disputes.
Our clear vision is to be accessible, fair, effective,
responsive, and accountable to the citizens of Florida.
Throughout the Fourteenth Judicial Circuit you
will find committed judges who uphold the integrity and independence
of the judiciary, perform their duties diligently and
impartially, and engage in activities designed
to improve the law and the administration of justice.
You will also find a team of skilled professionals
dedicated to expanding
the vision of our courts and maintaining an efficient Court
Administration. We aspire to the highest ethical standards and
professionalism in our performance both in and out of the courtroom.
The judges and staff constantly endeavor to uphold the
public trust and confidence in our court system.
We hope you find this site helpful as you
navigate your way through the judicial system.
Christopher N. Patterson, Chief Judge
Robyn Gable, Court Administrator
