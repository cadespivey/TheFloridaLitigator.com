A. Communications with the Judicial Office

Method of Communication: E-mail (preferred) and phone. Voicemail messages will be returned within 24 business hours where feasible.
Ex parte Communications: The Code of Judicial Conduct prohibits all judges from considering ex parte (one side only) communications. Judicial assistants are prohibited from making substantive inquiries of the judge on behalf of any attorney or litigant involved in any action.
Florida Courts E-Filing Portal: All attorneys and self-represented litigants must make and receive service by email, generally through the Florida Court E-Filing Portal, unless excused under Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.516. Pro se litigants unfamiliar with the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal should contact the Clerk’s Office for assistance.
Response to Inquiries: Within 24 business hours of receipt.

B. Scheduling Procedures

Court Schedule: Arraignments begin at 8:00 a.m. CST; Docket Day begins at 8:30 a.m. CST; Jury Selection begins at 8:00 a.m. CST.
Scheduling Hearings: The moving party is responsible for setting a motion for hearing. Please email the judicial assistant for the upcoming criminal motion hearing dates; coordinate with opposing counsel; notify the judicial assistant of the agreed-upon date and file the Notice of Hearing.
Notice of Hearing: It is the scheduling party’s responsibility to file the Notice of Hearing. If parties fail to coordinate, the Court may decline to hear the motion.
Submission Deadlines: Case law for consideration of a motion hearing should be e-filed through the Clerk’s Office as a Notice of Filing at least five (5) days prior to the scheduled hearing. Late-filed materials may not be considered.
Continuance Procedure: Requested by motion and proposed order submitted through the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal. Continuances will not be granted absent good cause shown. Obtain opposing party’s position in advance of filing the Motion.
Cancelling Hearings: Please cancel hearings by filing a Notice of Cancellation with copies to all parties.

C. Remote Appearance

Remote Appearance Procedure: Any person requesting to appear remotely (Zoom) must file a Motion through the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal and e-mail a copy of the motion to the judicial assistant. An order allowing appearance via Zoom will be entered by the Court. Non-evidentiary hearings may be permitted remotely by stipulation of the parties with Court approval.

Platform Used: https://zoom.us/j/2421533555PMI 242 153 3555

Requirements: Persons appearing by Zoom are responsible for securing a reliable internet connection and a device with audio-video capabilities. Participants must appear in a quiet, private location, free from distractions, with their face clearly visible at all times. Courtroom attire is required. If technical issues prevent participation, the responsibility lies with the party requesting remote appearance.
Defendants appearing via Zoom must display a valid, government-issued photo identification at the beginning of the proceedings for identity verification.

D. Submission of Orders and Judgments

Format: All proposed Orders and Judgments should be submitted in Word format to the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal. All Orders are date-stamped below the judge’s electronic signature. Please use the following signature line:

DONE AND ORDERED at Milton, Santa Rosa County, Florida.

Submission Method: Florida Courts E-Filing Portal.
Agreed Orders: May be submitted with a stipulation signed by both parties.
Competing Orders: If parties submit competing orders after a hearing, the Court will select or modify one as appropriate.

E. Courtesy Copies of Case Law and Other Documents

When Required: As needed.
Format: Case law filed through the E Florida Courts E-Filing Portal must be clean, unaltered copies. Courtesy copies provided directly to chambers may include highlights or tabs for ease of reference.
Submission Method: Filed through the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal as a Notice of Filing.
Deadline: Case law for consideration at a motion hearing should be provided at least five (5) days prior to the scheduled hearing.

F. Emergency and Other Urgent Matters

Requirements: E-mail judicial assistant with cc to opposing party. The Court will determine on a case-by-case basis whether a matter constitutes an emergency. Generally, an emergency involves circumstances requiring immediate judicial attention to prevent irreparable harm or to address urgent liberty or safety concerns. Parties requesting emergency treatment must clearly state in their motion the grounds for expedited handling and cite any applicable rule, statute, or administrative order.
Scheduling: Contact the judicial assistant by email with a copy to opposing parties. Same-day hearings may be set only if warranted, subject to the Court’s availability and consistent with governing rules or administrative orders.

G. Exhibits for Evidentiary Proceedings

Submission Method: Hard copy submitted in court. Digital exhibits (PDF/USB) may also be accepted with prior Court approval.
Format: All attorneys and self-represented litigants must provide copies of each exhibit for the clerk, the court, and each party to review during the trial or hearing.
Labeling: Exhibits must be pre-marked (e.g., 'Exhibit A,' 'Exhibit B') before submission.

H. Other Division Procedures

ADA Accommodations: Contact Court Administration to request ADA Accommodations at least seven (7) business days in advance of the proceeding, or as soon as practicable if the need become known later.
