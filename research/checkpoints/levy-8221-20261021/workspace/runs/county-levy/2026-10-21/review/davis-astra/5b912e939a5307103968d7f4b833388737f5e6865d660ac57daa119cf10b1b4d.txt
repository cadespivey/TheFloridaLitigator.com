Davis’ Division Procedures Alachua Circuit Civil & Levy Circuit Criminal Communications with the Judicial Office Method of Communication: All communications to the judicial office must be submitted by e-mail to PolkL@Circuit8.org . The subject line must contain the case number, case name, and relevant matter (e.g., 2024 CC 001234  – Doe v. Doe – 2-Hour Hearing Requested.) Ex parte Communications: All communications with the judicial office must comply with Canon 3 of the Code of Judicial Conduct, which prohibits a judge from initiating, permitting, or considering ex parte communications and from considering other communications outside the presence of the parties concerning a pending or impending proceeding, unless authorized by law. All parties must be copied on any e-mail directed to the judicial office, unless an ex parte communication is authorized by law. Unsolicited Communications: Unsolicited communications from non-parties will not be considered by the court. Only parties may contact the judicial office in accordance with these practices and procedures. E-Filing Portal Contact Information: All attorneys and self-represented litigants must provide an e-mail address to receive signed orders electronically, unless excused. Fla. R. Gen. Prac. & Jud. Admin. 2.516. It is the responsibility of attorneys and self-represented litigants to update their contact information using Form 2.603 any time there is a change in the e-mail account registered for electronic service. Response to Inquiries: Judicial Assistants are not authorized to provide legal advice. All messages on the telephone should be accompanied by an email message. If the Judicial Assistant is out of the office, an email response will be returned to the sender with information regarding which Judicial Assistant is providing coverage and the period of time coverage is being provided. Scheduling Procedures Court Schedule: The Judge’s scheduling calendars are not available online. Scheduling Hearings: Please email PolkL@circuit8.org to request hearing time. The email request should include: Case Number Style of Case A courtesy copy of the motion(s) being set The total amount of time requested for both sides Change of Plea: Changes of Plea should be set on the Criminal General docket unless specially set with JA. Any plea expected to last more than 15 minutes should be specially set on the judge’s calendar. Changes of Plea are not generally accepted at Case Management and should be set on the appropriate docket. Parties should have a completed plea petition with the applicable attachments at the time of the scheduled court event. Find applicable forms on the Forms and Checklist page . Notice of Hearing: A notice of hearing must be filed and served immediately after receiving hearing time. A hearing is not set on the Court’s calendar unless you receive confirmation from the Judicial Assistant. A notice of hearing involving any remote appearance must list the Judicial Zoom credentials.  All notices of hearing must contain the ADA notification required by Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.540.  A courtesy copy of the notice of hearing shall be emailed to the Judicial Assistant. Submission Deadlines: The Court no longer accepts exhibits by e-mail. All exhibits should be sent to Judge Davis’ office  in a binder no later than 5 days before the hearing. Order of Proceedings: Parties represented by attorneys will generally be heard first. Continuance Procedure: Continuances are disfavored and will be granted only upon good cause shown. A request for continuance must be submitted at least seven days prior to the scheduled court date. Except for good cause shown, the motion must be signed by the party requesting the continuance, as required by Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.545(e). Cancelling Hearings: You must cancel hearings by notifying the judicial assistant immediately. You must also immediately file and serve a notice of cancellation on the opposing counsel and any self-represented litigant with a courtesy copy provided to the Judicial Assistant. Other Scheduling Procedures: Once a motion or petition is scheduled, any other motions or matters may not be noticed at the same time without prior approval from the Judicial Assistant. Remote Appearance Remote Appearance Procedure: Remote appearances are permitted for Case Management in the early stages of the case, but the Court may require in person appearances. Other Zoom appearances may be permitted at the discretion of the Court. Platform Used: The court uses Zoom for remote appearances. Platform Meeting ID#: Contact the judicial assistant to obtain the platform meeting ID#. Decorum and Attire: Although the hearing is being conducted virtually, proper courtroom decorum should be maintained. Participants should be in a quiet setting and minimize any external distractions. Be aware of what is behind you and choose a solid neutral wall, if possible. Please dress like you are coming to the courthouse and conduct yourself as you would in person. Submissions of Orders and Judgements Format: All proposed orders must be submitted through the Florida Courts E-Filing portal in Microsoft Word format.  All orders shall have a 2” by 3.5″ blank space on the first page of the order in the upper right-hand corner.  All proposed orders must be accompanied by a cover letter either (1) certifying that all parties agree to the order or (2) containing a statement identifying any disagreement of the parties as to the proposed order.  All orders shall include a certificate of service at the bottom of the order.  Please indicate the name of the party who is to receive the order, as well as that party’s email/e-service address and mailing address. A courtesy copy of the filed motion shall be emailed to the Judicial Assistant at polkl@circuit8.org Submission Method: All proposed orders must be submitted through the Florida Courts E-Filing portal OR via email to polkl@circuit8.org Deadline for Submissions: Proposed orders must be submitted within 10 days after any hearing, unless specifically ordered to be submitted within a shorter period of time. Courtesy Copies of Case Law and Other Documents When Required: Courtesy copies of case law must be submitted to the court three days prior to the hearing for any evidentiary proceeding. Emergency and Other Urgent Matters Requirements: If a party believes there is a factual basis for setting an emergency hearing, a detailed motion setting forth the following must be filed: (1) the issues to be resolved, (2) reasons why an emergency hearing is necessary, and (3) the amount of time needed for each party’s presentation. Scheduling: If the court determines that an emergency exists, a hearing will be scheduled unilaterally by the court. All parties shall make themselves available for emergency hearing, barring exigent circumstances. Exhibits for Evidentiary Proceedings Submission Method: All exhibits should be pre-marked with a letter for identification. An Index of Exhibits shall be included with an indication of whether each exhibit has or has not been stipulated to by the parties.  Please email polkl@circuit8.org to make arrangements to drop off your binder(s).  Exhibits must be labeled in the following format: Petitioner/Plaintiff 1 or Respondent/Defendant 1. Deadline for Submissions: The Court no longer accepts exhibits by e-mail. All exhibits should be sent to Judge Davis’ office (Room 409) in a binder no later than 5 days before the hearing. Pretrial Procedures and Conferences Case Management Conferences and Hearing on Pending Motions: Any party may request a case management conference (CMC) when a case requires. The court strongly encourages the early use of CMC in more complex cases, multiple-party litigation, or any case that might benefit from court intervention. Unless excused by the court in advance, all CMCs are mandatory for attorneys, self-represented litigants, and parties. Status Conference: Any party may request a status conference when a case requires.  Unless excused by the court in advance, all Status Conferences are mandatory for attorneys, self-represented litigants, and parties. Scheduling: If the court agrees that a CMC or Status Conference is required, the moving party may schedule the CMC or Status Conference through the Judicial Assistant. Forms Find any applicable forms on the Forms and Checklists page . Other Division Procedures ADA Accommodations: If you need ADA accommodation, please contact the ADA Coordinator, Alachua County Family and Civil Justice Center, 201 East University Avenue, Gainesville, FL 32601, phone# 352 337 6237 at least 7 days prior to your scheduled Court appearance or immediately upon receiving the notification if the time before the scheduled appearance is less than 7 days.  If you are hearing of voice-impaired, please call 1 800 955 8770 via Florida Relay Service.
