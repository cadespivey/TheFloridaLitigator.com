THE EIGHTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT OF FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 1.34 
ORDER APPOINTING ROBERT E. FOLSOM AS SPECIAL MASTER FOR BELATED 
APPEAL PROCEEDINGS IN THE EIGHTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT 
WHEREAS, the First District Court of Appeal frequently requests the Chief Judge of the 
circuit to appoint a special master to serve as a commissioner for that Court in belated appeal 
proceedings pursuant to Florida Rule of Appellate Procedure 9.141(c) and Staley v. State, 12 So. 
3d 778 (Fla. 1st DCA 2009); and, 
WHEREAS, it would be in the best interest of the circuit to appoint a single special 
master to preside over all belated appeal proceedings arising from cases within the circuit; 
NOW THEREFORE, by the authority vested in me as the Chief Judge of the Eighth Judicial 
Circuit, and pursuant to the Florida Rules of Judicial Administration,  
IT IS ORDERED that: 
Robert E. Folsom, Esq., is hereby appointed to serve as Special Master for all rule 
9.141(c) belated appeal proceedings in the Eighth Judicial Circuit, including those arising from 
petitions seeking a belated appeal from county court to circuit court.  This appointment shall 
remain in effect until further Order of the Court.   
DONE AND ORDERED at Gainesville, Alachua County, Florida, this 12th day of February 
2018. 
Toby S. Monaco, Chief Judge
