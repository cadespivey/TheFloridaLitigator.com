THE EIGHTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT OF FLORIDA 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 3.06 
RETENTION OF MORTGAGE FORECLOSURE DOCUMENTS 
FILED IN CIVIL CASES 
In order to ensure that paper versions of original notes, mortgages and lost note bonds 
filed in civil cases are retained by the Clerks of Court in all counties within the Eighth Judicial 
Circuit; and 
WHEREAS, Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.525(a) defines 
the “Official Court File” as “a set of electronic documents docketed and stored in a computer 
system maintained by the clerk under this rule. Documents in the official court file are originals 
for all purposes except as otherwise provided by statute or rule;” and 
WHEREAS, with regard to the foreclosure of mortgages, section 702.015(4), Florida 
Statutes, requires that “the original note and the allonges must be filed with the court before the 
entry of any judgment of foreclosure or judgment on the note.” and 
WHEREAS, Florida Rule of General Practice and Judicial Administration 2.525(c)(2) states 
that unless prohibited by order, rule, or statute, after conversion to an electronic form, the clerk 
will dispose of paper documents submitted for filing or return them to the filer if the filer provides 
a self-addressed postage-prepaid envelope to the clerk at the time of submission.  
WHEREAS, in order to maintain the paper version of the original note as required by 
statute, an order is needed requiring such paper versions of any note or allonge to be retained 
as part of the court file until such time as the property subject to the mortgage foreclosure 
proceeding is sold. 
IT IS THEREFORE  
ORDERED that: 
The Clerks of Court in the Eighth Judicial Circuit must retain all mortgages, notes, and lost 
note bonds filed in civil cases. The Clerk shall not destroy, return, or send any of these 
instruments to any person absent a specific order by the Court, unless otherwise provided in this 
order. Such documents may be disposed in the same manner as other paper filings 90 days after
