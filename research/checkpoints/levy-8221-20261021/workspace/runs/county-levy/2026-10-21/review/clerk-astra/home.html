Levy County Clerk of Court and Comptroller

Our team is honored to serve the citizens of Levy County and the greater community that relies on our valued programs and services. Whether online or in person, our goal is to provide exceptional service to the public through our core values of Respect, Integrity, Service, & Trust.

As the elected Clerk of the Circuit Court and Comptroller for Levy County, Matt Brooks is dedicated to the management and safeguarding of all county records and financial resources.
