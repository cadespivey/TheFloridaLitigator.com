About the Clerk

Serving Putnam County

Matt Reynolds was elected Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller of Putnam County in 2020.

The Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller is a duly elected Constitutional Officer as mandated by Article V, Section 16 of the Constitution of the State of Florida and is a member of the Florida Association of Court Clerks and Comptrollers.

Clerk Reynolds can be reached at (386) 326-7603 or [email protected].

Experience

Mr. Reynolds has been a resident of Putnam County since he was just a few days old. He graduated with honors from Palatka High School, St. John’s River State College, and the University of North Florida. He earned a degree in business administration with a major in Finance from the University of North Florida.

Matt previously served as the Deputy County Administrator for the Putnam County Board of County Commissioners. Previously, he was the Finance Director for the City of Palatka for almost a decade. He also served as the Assistant City Manager and Interim City Manager while at the City of Palatka.

Community Involvement

Matt has been registered as a Republican since he was able to register to vote. He and his family live in Palatka, and they actively attend Trinity Baptist Church.​​​​​​​

Organizations & Boards

Matt is active in the community and is a member of the Board of Directors for multiple local organizations such as:

  1. Rotary Club of Palatka

  2. First Coast Community Credit Union

  3. Rodeheaver Boys Ranch

  4. ARC of Putnam County

  5. Children Reading Center Charter School

He has been, or is currently, the treasurer of a majority of these organizations, as well as the Audit Committee Chairman for First Coast Community Credit Union.​​​​​​​
