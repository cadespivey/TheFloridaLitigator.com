FM-2026-004-SC (A)
IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF FLORIDA, SEVENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT IN AND FOR FLAGLER, PUTNAM, ST. JOHNS AND VOLUSIA COUNTIES
Re: Appointment of General Magistrates and Child Support Enforcement Hearing Officers
Ref: FM-2026-004-SC( Rescinds FM-2026-004-SC)
WHEREAS,
the Seventh Judicial Circuit of Florida utilizes general magistrates and child support enforcement hearing officers;
NOW THEREFORE, I, LEAH R. CASE,
Chief Judge of the Seventh Judicial Circuit of Florida, hereby order as follows:
Alexander Alvarez, Sharon Feliciano, Stacey Kircher, and Denise Mensh are appointed (or reappointed) to serve as General Magistrates and Child Support Enforcement Hearing Officers in the Seventh Judicial Circuit of Florida.
The general powers and duties of general magistrates and child support enforcement hearing officers are set forth in Rules 12.490(c) and 12.491(e), Florida Family Law Rules of Procedure, and Rule 8.257(c), Florida Rules of Juvenile Procedure.
Administrative oversight of general magistrates and child support enforcement hearing officers has been delegated by the undersigned to the Court Administrator or his/her designee.
TO BE RECORDED
in Flagler, Putnam, St. Johns, and Volusia counties.
DONE AND ORDERED
in Daytona Beach, Volusia County, Florida, this
9th
day of October 2026.
/s/ Leah R. Case
Leah R. Case
Chief Judge
