IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE FIFTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT IN AND FOR 
CITRUS, HERNANDO, LAKE, MARION AND SUMTER COUNTIES 
ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER A-2025-35 
IN RE: APPOINTMENT OF SYLVIA SIMMONS AS A CHILD SUPPORT 
ENFORCEMENT HEARING OFFICER 
WHEREAS, utilization of child support enforcement hearing officer in the Fifth Judicial 
Circuit or Florida is set forth in Administrative Order A-2020-08, the amendments thereof, or 
the administrative order which may replace A-2020-08; and 
WHEREAS, it has become necessary to appoint Sylvia Simmons as a Child Support 
Enforcement Hearing Officer pursuant to Florida Family Law Rule of Procedure 12.491; 
IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED: 
1.
Sylvia Simmons is appointed to serve as a Child Support Enforcement Hearing Officer in
the Fifth Judicial Circuit of Florida.
2.
As a Child Support Enforcement Hearing Officer, Ms. Simmons shall have the power and
the duty to:
a) Schedule, prepare for and conduct hearings in Title IV child support establishment
and enforcement cases including 48 hour writ reviews on Title IV-D cases (unless
otherwise directed by the Administrative Judge); conduct research: prepare orders;
travel to outlying counties or courthouses to conduct such hearings; administrative
duties directly related to these activities; and any other activity specified in 45
C.F.R.§ 304.20 that is relevant to the duties of a hearing. officer.
3.
Upon conclusion of the hearing the Child Support Hearing Officer shall file the report
and serve copies on the parties pursuant to Florida Family Rules of Procedure 12.491(e)(4).
The Child Support Hearing Officers shall include with the report a proposed order.
4.
The Child Support Hearing Officer shall take testimony and establish a record by electronic
means pursuant to 2.535, Florida Rules of General Practice and Judicial Administration. The record
will be made, retained, and destroyed in accordance with Rule of Judicial Administration 2.430
and any administrative orders governing court reporting in the Fifth Circuit.
5.
All actions taken before this date by this or any child support hearing officer are hereby
confirmed.
6.
This Order shall take effect 12:01 am. August 1, 2025, and will remain in full force and effect
unless amended, rescinded, superseded, or replaced.
