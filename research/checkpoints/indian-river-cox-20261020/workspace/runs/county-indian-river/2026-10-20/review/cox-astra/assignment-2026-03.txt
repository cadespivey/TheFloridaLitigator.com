JUDICIAL ASSIGNMENTS FOR 2026 
WHEREAS, Florida Rules of General Practice & Administration 2.215 states that the Chief 
Judge shall assign judges to the courts and divisions, and shall determine the length of each assignment; 
NOW, THEREFORE, pursuant to the authority conferred by Florida Rules of General Practice 
& Administration 2.215, it is ORDERED the assignments for the judges in the Nineteenth Judicial 
Circuit shall be as follows, EFFECTIVE JANUARY 12, 2026:
