PROBATE/GUARDIANSHIP PROCEDURES FOR  
NASSAU COUNTY1 
 
 
All probate and guardianship matters are handled by Senior Judge 
Robert M. Foster. Judge Foster typically handles probate hearings one 
Monday per month. It is the Court’s preference that proposed orders be 
submitted via the e-portal. Instructions on how to submit proposed orders 
via the e-portal can be found at https://www.jud4.org/proposed-orders-
efiling  
 
Senior Judges do not have assistants. Sarah Meeks, Judge 
Charbula’s assistant, will primarily handle the processing of 
proposed orders (via the portal). Ashley Strickland, Judge Fahlgren’s 
assistant, will primarily handle the scheduling of hearings. To secure 
a hearing date, please email Ashley Strickland at astrickland@coj.net. 
Judge Foster’s preference is that hearings be conducted via Zoom at the 
link listed below. In-person hearings are generally held in the Grand Jury 
Room on the third floor of the Robert M. Foster Justice Center.
