Protecting taxpayer dollars takes more than balancing the books.

Comptrollers conduct independent audits and financial reviews to help ensure county funds are spent according to the law.

This includes:
✅Pre-auditing expenditures before payment
✅Reviewing contracts
✅Conducting internal post-audits
✅Evaluating financial controls
✅Preparing reports with recommendations for improvement

Strong financial oversight helps promote accountability, transparency, and responsible stewardship of public funds.

COLUMBIA COUNTY, FLORIDA
CLERK OF COURT & COMPTROLLER

James M. Swisher, Jr, Clerk of Court for Columbia County, Florida is a duly elected Constitutional Officer. The Clerk of the Circuit Court is an elected office established according to the Florida Constitution for the purpose of serving the citizens of each county as a public trustee. As an elected official, the Clerk of the Circuit Court helps provide for the system of “checks and balances” that is the very foundation of the American form of government.
