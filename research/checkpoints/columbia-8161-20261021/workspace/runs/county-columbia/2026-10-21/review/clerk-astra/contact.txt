YOUR FEEDBACK IS IMPORTANT

We welcome your comments and suggestions regarding the Columbia County Clerk of Court. Your feedback is very important to us, not only in our continued efforts to improve your experience but also to provide you with excellent customer service. Please do not contact the Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller’s office by email regarding court cases. Please contact our office by phone.
