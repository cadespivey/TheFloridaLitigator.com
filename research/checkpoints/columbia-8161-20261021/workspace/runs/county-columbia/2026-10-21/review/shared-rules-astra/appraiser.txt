To All Residents of Columbia County

Our Fort White Office is open every Wednesday year-round.

If you have not filed for homestead or any other exemption the statutory deadline is March 1st. If you have any extenuating circumstances that prevent you from meeting that deadline please contact our office.

If you have filed for the Senior Citizen exemption and we have not verified your income please give us a call for further instructions.

The Tangible Personal Property deadline is April 1st.

Our office has taken the necessary steps to protect our employees and citizens. If you are still uncomfortable with coming to our office please call us at 386-758-1083 and we will make arrangements to assist you.
