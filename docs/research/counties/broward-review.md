## Material civil-procedure examples

These are summaries of retained source statements, not case-specific advice or
a determination that one source controls another.

- **Courthouse assignment:** the saved [AO 2025-40-CO](https://www.17th.flcourts.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/AO-2025-40-CO.pdf)
  uses the primary street address of the attorney's physical office for represented
  plaintiffs and the plaintiff's ZIP code for unrepresented plaintiffs. It also
  states missing-ZIP, co-counsel and anti-circumvention provisions. Insurance-only
  defendant cases and specified consumer-debt/credit-card categories have separate
  treatment; the differing category wording is preserved rather than generalized.
  The order states issuance on October 20, 2025; no separate effective date was
  recorded. A [firm-specific filing exception](https://www.17th.flcourts.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/2023-22-CO.pdf)
  is retained separately. This baseline does not implement a venue calculator.
- **Judge-specific practice:** records distinguish hearing scheduling, conferral,
  motion and proposed-order channels, courtesy copies, remote attendance, exhibits
  and trial instructions. CMS submission, clerk filing and service are separate
  actions. Differing deadlines and outdated references are preserved with sources.
- **Property and foreclosure:** reviewed findings cover the first-party property-loss
  order and forms, property-fraud procedures, foreclosure procedures and worksheets,
  and clerk operational instructions. The auction provider's unavailable content
  remains a gap; no bank or wire instructions were inferred.
- **Shared administration:** relevant filing, fees, evidence handling, arbitration,
  mediation, court reporting, courthouse access and accommodation materials have
  scoped records. Historical orders and express supersession/vacatur statements
  retain their dates rather than being reported as newly adopted requirements.
