# Active goal: statewide evidence baseline and reusable research workflow

## Objective and authority

Complete and validate an evidence-backed personnel, contact and practical
civil/commercial-procedure baseline for all 67 Florida counties. Preserve the
Florida Supreme Court and six DCA rosters, entire Florida Statutes compilation,
procedural baseline and five finalized historical reports. Build and validate the
reusable agentic research workflow through this work, with explicit source maps,
search instructions, persistence steps and validation for later agents.

Astra executes research, implementation, validation and corrections directly.
No local-worker delegation, inference-server restart or model unloading. Continue
until the full completion audit passes; do not pause after each county. Finish
one person's supported record and save it before moving to another. Federal courts
remain deferred on the [expansion roadmap](EXPANSION_ROADMAP.md).

## Scope and evidence requirements

- Reconcile each official county roster, including applicable circuit/county
  judges, chief and senior coverage, magistrates/hearing officers, and clerk.
  Distinguish regular local assignments from circuit-wide or temporary authority.
- Preserve names, assignments, office/chambers contacts, judicial-assistant names
  and contacts, official profiles, individual procedure links and applicable
  material shared rules. Do not substitute generic footer contacts for personal
  contacts. Every required field needs evidence or a documented bounded-search gap.
- Prioritize civil procedure, jurisdiction, contracts, business entities,
  commercial transactions, property, foreclosure, collections, remedies, evidence,
  appellate practice and relevant court administration. Record concise structured
  operating checklists plus summaries: scheduling, conferral, submissions,
  proposed orders, courtesy copies, remote appearances and communications.
- For family, criminal, juvenile, diversion and other noncommercial assignments,
  contacts, assignment, official links and basic operating instructions suffice.
  No exhaustive specialty manuals, individual litigation, motion recipes or case
  deadlines. Administrative orders require substantive review only when materially
  relevant to civil procedure, filing location, access or shared administration.
- Save original bytes, extraction, URL, actual retrieval timestamp, hashes and
  stated revision/effective dates (or unknown). Keep collected, partially reviewed,
  reviewed and unavailable material distinct. Reuse existing valid originals with
  their original dates. A first observation is not a new legal development.
- Preserve conflicting statements with source evidence and a brief discrepancy
  note; do not decide which controls. A documented conflict or intentionally limited
  noncivil review does not block publication. Inaccuracy and undisclosed missing
  evidence require correction.
- Preserve five finalized reports and their supported historical limitations.
  Historical snapshots cover July 1, then changes through August 1, September 1,
  October 1 and October 11, 2026. Do not backdate current roster observations.
  Regular weekly periods are Monday–Sunday. Compare against the last finalized
  report by default, or an explicit start date; partial research never advances it.

## Execution and persistence

Use [the county workflow](COUNTY_WORKFLOW.md) and the relevant source map in
`docs/research/counties/`. Before new retrieval, state the missing required field
or material operating requirement. Search saved county/circuit evidence first,
then the official roster, profile and linked guide; use targeted official-domain
search only for remaining gaps. Record actual searches and outcomes.

Follow [the checkpoint contract](BASELINE_CAPTURE.md),
[review export](COUNTY_REVIEW_EXPORT.md) and
[directory publication](COUNTY_DIRECTORY_PUBLICATION.md). Save immutable findings
and originals in the shared database; verify readback, hashes and exact replay.
Use reviewed supplements for current directory corrections. Never alter a
historical report to publish a contact update. Preserve unrelated working files.

## Completion audit — all requirements must be demonstrated

1. All 67 county rosters are reconciled to official evidence. Required fields,
   individual links, bounded procedures and shared civil/access rules are supported
   or have honest source-specific gaps. Each county has reviewed acceptance evidence.
2. Full Supreme Court/DCA roster, assistant/clerk contacts and applicable procedural
   coverage remain supported. Statutory compilation coverage and source identity
   are reconciled; collection does not imply exhaustive substantive review.
3. Findings, originals, review scope, conflicts, dates and gaps persist correctly;
   exact replay/readback and original hashes pass. History and comparison semantics
   remain intact, including finalized-report and explicit-start-date baselines.
4. Reviewed current directories are published and checked live, including numerical
   court ordering, county navigation, readable contacts, source links, responsive
   layout and historical isolation. Five supported reports and their limitations
   remain intact; date selectors and source links work as specified.
5. Later agents can use current instructions, county source maps and exercised code
   to resume, collect, save, compare and publish. Identify manual steps accurately;
   synthetic tests and design proposals are not proof of live execution.
6. The 67-county coverage/gap matrix and requirement-level audit identify evidence
   for each claim, unresolved limitations and completed corrections. Finish remaining
   corrections and publication; do not relabel partial scope as complete.
