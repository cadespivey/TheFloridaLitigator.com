## Agreed scope and administrative-order screen

For each county, include all county judges and circuit judges assigned to serve
it; divisions/assignments; judicial assistants; published chambers contacts;
clerk and relevant civil court-administration contacts; official individual
procedure links; and court, official-records, court-records and property-appraiser
navigation links. Review substantive civil responsibilities and shared procedures.
Keep the existing commercial civil priority and limited criminal/diversion review.

Administrative orders require substantive review only when they significantly
affect procedure, courthouse access, or are otherwise highly relevant to civil
litigators. Examples include geographic or ZIP-code filing/assignment requirements,
filing restrictions, hearing practices, emergency access, and AI-use/disclosure
requirements. Routine internal administration and unrelated programs do not require
exhaustive review. Screen official indexes and relevant notices, opening documents
when titles or descriptions do not establish applicability. Record a short reason
for inclusion, exclusion, or unresolved relevance; a screened index is not a claim
that every order was substantively reviewed.

The user's Broward ZIP-code filing example and possible statewide supersession
of local AI orders are discovery leads, not verified current legal requirements.
Do not assume whose address determines filing location or that all AI orders were
superseded. Verify exact text, affected cases, dates, and official later action.

For included orders, check official amendment/rescission indexes, successor orders,
and relevant Florida Supreme Court rulemaking/orders. Record express repeal,
replacement, modification, or supersession with the supporting text and effective
date. Retain the original and successor documents. Do not present an expressly
superseded requirement as current after the supported effective date. If sources
disagree without an express resolution, preserve both statements and mark the
status discrepancy without deciding which controls. A recorded conflict does not
block completion of otherwise accurate, scoped source review.

Preserve original documents, source URLs, retrieval timestamps, stated revision
and effective dates, and separate collection/review/conflict states. Newly found
existing requirements are baseline observations, not automatically legal changes.
Share circuit evidence across applicable counties without expanding the personnel
scope to every county in that circuit. Missing evidence remains a gap.
